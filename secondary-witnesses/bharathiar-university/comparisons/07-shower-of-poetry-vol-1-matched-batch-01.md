## 1 — O Panneerselvam! → repository item 39 `பன்னீர்ச்செல்வமே!`

**Result: MATCH CONFIRMED / repository text remains source-preferred.**

Title difference is minor: BU **O Panneerselvam!** versus repository **Panneerselvam!**. The body is unquestionably the same youthful elegy for A. T. Panneerselvam.

High-value differences:

- BU introduces **“tricks and frauds”** into the opening dark-room image. The Tamil instead has the image of people said to be rolling about in a dark room with both eyes closed (`இருட்டறையில் இரு கண்கள் மூடிக் கொண்டு / உருட்டுகின்றார் ...`); the repository preserves that image rather than importing fraud language.
- `அறிவியக்க வீரன்` is interpreted by BU as a **hero of the Rationalist Movement**. The repository's **warrior of the knowledge movement** stays closer to the lexical source and leaves the political inference implicit.
- Most importantly, BU renders source `பஞ்சைகளல்ல நாங்கள்` as **“We are not cowards.”** In context `பஞ்சைகள்` denotes paupers / destitute people, not cowards. Repository **“We are not paupers”** is source-supported and must stand.
- Several compressed phrases flatten the source's ripe-fruit / cool-mist / sea imagery but do not expose a repository defect.

**Classification:** interpretive smoothing + one strong BU semantic mistranslation (`பஞ்சைகள்` → cowards).  
Tamil correction candidate: **0**. Released-English correction candidate: **0**.

## 8 — The Beloved Son of the Muse of Arts → item 40 `கலைத்தாயின் தலைச் செல்வன்!`

**Result: MATCH CONFIRMED / substantial pun-loss in BU.**

The identity was hardened in Report 06. This payload pass confirms the same N. S. Krishnan / Kalaivanar elegy throughout.

High-value differences:

- BU **The Beloved Son of the Muse of Arts** weakens source `தலைச் செல்வன்` from a foremost / chief son to merely a beloved son. Repository **Mother Art’s Foremost Son!** preserves the source hierarchy better.
- The source's cinema-title wordplay is flattened. `நல்ல தம்பி`, `பணம்`, and `மணமகள்` are not preserved by BU as the film-title sequence **Nalla Thambi / Panam / Manamagal**; repository English explicitly retains those titles.
- BU turns the `பணம்` passage into a general statement about men scheming for huge sums and the poet-singer giving liberally, losing the specific title pun.
- BU renders source `கிந்தன்` as **“Kandan”** in the old-Nandan / new-Kindan contrast. Repository retains **Kindan**, the source-controlled name / pun.
- BU adds contextual colouring such as **Communist Russia** where the Tamil simply gives `ருசியா`; this is an interpretive expansion, not source authority.

**Classification:** title interpretation + structural condensation + film-title pun loss + name/transliteration drift.  
Tamil correction candidate: **0**. Released-English correction candidate: **0**.

## 11 — Freedom Fighters → item 6 `விடுதலை வீரர்கள்`

**Result: MATCH CONFIRMED / materially condensed secondary translation.**

BU preserves the 15-08-1967 Tiruchirappalli Radio setting, Kattabomman, the Marudhu brothers, Bharati, V. V. S. Aiyar, V. O. C. and the final freedom-for-all argument.

Principal differences:

- The opening address and several source metaphors are shortened or generalized.
- The Kattabomman `கயிறு` / Kayathar wordplay is retained only in a very literal, awkward English form.
- Guest-poet transitions are substantially compressed compared with the FINAL-CLEARED Tamil and repository English.
- The closing gold / jewellery / rose image is reduced to a skeleton: BU retains freedom-as-pure-gold and the monkey contrast but drops much of the source's `பத்தரை மாற்று`, craft/jewellery, sword-bearing warriors and adorned-women architecture.
- The source-visible argument that even poetry lacking *yāppu* may stand so long as land, language, honour and feeling do not go unprotected is present, but more compressed than in the repository.

No BU wording provides source-backed reason to reopen the Tamil or released English.

**Classification:** heavy condensation + rhetorical smoothing.  
Tamil correction candidate: **0**. Released-English correction candidate: **0**.

## 12 — The Five Senses → item 7 `ஐம்புலன்`

**Result: MATCH CONFIRMED / source wordplay is repeatedly flattened.**

BU retains the 24-03-1968 Vaniyambadi setting, nose-injury invitation joke, five-sense debate, large-body / owl / capsicum / chatterbox / winnowing-tray sequence, poet hand-offs and the closing moral.

High-value differences:

- The elaborate opening address and several friendship / invitation images are compressed.
- Tirukkural scaffolding in the body, eye, nose and ear debates is reduced compared with the repository.
- The Tamil closing depends on `மெய்` meaning both **body** and **truth**. BU closes with an English statement that the body would not be “a body true, but only a false one,” conveying the idea but losing the lexical double meaning. Repository explicitly preserves **mey / truth** and **poi / falsehood** so the source wordplay remains visible.
- Other mouth/eye/nose/ear puns are similarly smoothed rather than reproduced.

**Classification:** wordplay loss + structural condensation; no source conflict.

## 13 — The New Path → item 11 `புதிய பாதை`

Principal findings:

- BU omits almost the entire extended river-name love sequence in which Kaveri / Kollidam / Vaigai / Manniyar / Kallanai / Palar / Periyar and related names become romantic wordplay. This is a large omission, not a repository interpolation.

- ADT-27, IR-8 and the crop-poet sequence survive, but in shortened form; the `durai` / Aduthurai political-language pun is only partly recoverable in BU.
- The government-policy conclusion is retained but shortened.

**Classification:** major omission + agricultural/political wordplay loss.

## 15 — Bharathidasan → item 14 `பாரதிதாசன்`

**Result: MATCH CONFIRMED / extensive editorial compression.**

BU keeps the Bombay setting, Bharati/Bharathidasan relationship, major quotations, social/political themes, afterlife conversation and guest-poet structure, but shortens the work materially.

High-value findings:

- The opening loses several exact similes and source wordplays while preserving general praise.
- The repository's source-sensitive Bharati / Bharathidasan name, `Mahakavi` / `மா` and literary-title play is more fully retained than BU's smoother prose-verse.
- BU compresses the bibliography of Bharathidasan works; the source's fuller chain including *Family Lamp*, *Isai Amudhu*, *Pandiyan Parisu*, *Kurinji Thittu*, *Kuyil* and *Azhagin Sirippu* is not preserved in full.
- The long comic **Muthusamy → Muthamma dream sequence** is omitted before BU moves directly to Muthusamy's “Revolution” presentation. This is a substantial source-structure omission.
- Several poet introductions and social-policy jokes are reduced, including wordplay that repository English retains with Tamil terms where required.

No BU compression supplies evidence for removing anything from the FINAL-CLEARED source text.

**Classification:** major editorial condensation + literary-title / comic-wordplay loss.

## 18 — Calculation - I → item 18 `கணக்கு`

**Result: MATCH CONFIRMED / strongest BU mistranslation cluster in this batch.**

The identity is certain from the 02-08-1970 Salem Tamil Sangam footnote and the same multiplication → subtraction → division → addition architecture. BU, however, compresses eight repository source scans into five printed pages and flattens much of the wordplay.

Strong diagnostic differences:

1. Source `இருபதும் பதினெட்டும் கூட்டிச் சொல்லல் மணக்கணக்கு` is **marriage calculation/account**. BU renders it **“Adding twenty to eighteen is mental calculation.”** This confuses `மணக்கணக்கு` with “mental calculation.” Repository **marriage account** remains source-supported.
2. In `மான் போட்ட கணக்குகளை வேங்கை அழித்துவிடும்`, BU gives **lion** for `வேங்கை`; repository preserves the tiger image.
3. Source `ஆற்றின் பெருக்கல் புனலாகும்` makes the **river's multiplication / swelling become floodwater**. BU changes this to **“When rains multiply, they become floods.”** Repository keeps the river anchor.
4. The accountant / son / marriage pun (`கணக்குப் பிள்ளை ... மணக்கப், பிள்ளை ...`) is heavily flattened.
5. The Salem / Servarayan / mango passage and the guest-poet introductions to Subtraction, Division and Addition are sharply condensed; much of the source's political and lexical play disappears.
6. The closing Tirukkural parody survives only in simplified form.

These are defects of the BU secondary translation, not evidence against the repository.

**Classification:** heavy condensation + multiple clear semantic/lexical mistranslations.  
Tamil correction candidate: **0**. Released-English correction candidate: **0**.
