## 19 — The Democracy that Nehru Found → item 19 `நேரு கண்ட ஜனநாயகம்`

**Result: MATCH CONFIRMED / major source-structure omission in BU.**

The title choice is interpretive: BU uses **The Democracy that Nehru Found**, while repository **Democracy as Nehru Saw It** keeps `கண்ட` in a memorial/perspective sense. Both identify the same 14-11-1970 Nehru kaviyarangam.

The most important difference is structural. After the opening greetings, BU moves rapidly into Nehru's freedom-struggle character. It omits the repository/source's long **flower-garden / bereaved Rose allegory** in which champak, jasmine, chrysanthemum and mullai seek the grieving Rose, and Rose laments the Nehru who once wore her upon his heart. That allegory occupies a substantial source-controlled block and is not repository expansion.

BU otherwise preserves the secular/religious-will material, non-alignment, Poongundranar, Anna's grief, Kalinga/Ashoka peace movement and the five-rights poet sequence, but often in smoother and shorter form. The source-visible `நேர் / நேரு / நேரும்` and `வாக்கு / வாக்குரிமை` sound architecture is also flattened.

**Classification:** major omission + smoothing + wordplay loss.  
Tamil correction candidate: **0**. Released-English correction candidate: **0**.

## 21 — The Silver Jubilee → item 21 `வெள்ளி விழா`

**Result: MATCH CONFIRMED / two strong BU errors; repository remains source-correct.**

The freedom-fighter sequence is substantially present: Bharati, V. O. C., V. V. S. Aiyar, Subramania Siva, Champakaraman Pillai, Vanchinathan, Tiruppur Kumaran and the unnamed martyrs.

Two high-confidence BU defects are source-diagnosable:

1. BU's closing says it looks forward to an incomparable **“Diamond Jubilee.”** The FINAL-CLEARED Tamil explicitly has `ஒப்பிலாப் பொன்விழா` — **Golden Jubilee**. Repository **Golden Jubilee** is source-controlled.
2. BU's book footnote dates the All India Radio / Rajaji Hall Silver Jubilee kaviyarangam as **10-08-1922**. The controlling Tamil work is explicitly the Silver Jubilee of independence and the repository source record gives **15-08-1972**. The BU footnote is therefore a later metadata error, not evidence for changing repository chronology.

Other differences are mostly shortening of guest-poet introductions and rhetorical imagery.

**Classification:** largely faithful condensation + one semantic mistranslation (`பொன்விழா` → Diamond Jubilee) + one secondary metadata error.  
Tamil correction candidate: **0**. Released-English correction candidate: **0**.

## 26 — In the Path of Anna → item 30 `அண்ணா வழியில்`

**Result: MATCH CONFIRMED / major omitted block plus one strong semantic mistranslation.**

BU preserves the elegiac opening, Anna's Tamil language reforms, equality/rationalism frame, personal memories, lamp image and closing prayer.

Two particularly important differences:

1. The source says `ஒன்றே குலம் ஒருவனே தேவன் - இதை / ஒப்புவதே உண்மை ஆத்திகமாம்!`. BU renders this as acceptance being true **“agnosticism.”** `ஆத்திகம்` is **theism/belief**, not agnosticism. Repository **true theism** is source-supported.
2. BU omits a large source-controlled middle block: the Kamban/Ravana rationalist interrogation (Menaka/Indra examples) and the playful chain woven from Anna-associated work titles such as **One Night, Moonrise, Good Younger Brother, Servant Maid, Chandramohan, Flame of Love, Gateway to Heaven, Kambarasam, Kumarik Kottam** and **Needhidevan Mayakkam**.

BU also generalizes `பூதகி` to a female demon and loses several Sanskrit→Tamil public-language contrasts and the `கணையாழி` / arrow-ring and Saturn-transit wordplay.

**Classification:** major omission + strong BU semantic error (`ஆத்திகம்` → agnosticism) + literary-title / language-reform wordplay loss.  
Tamil correction candidate: **0**. Released-English correction candidate: **0**.
