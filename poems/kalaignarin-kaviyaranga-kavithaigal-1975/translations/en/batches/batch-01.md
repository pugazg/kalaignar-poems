## Translator / source-sensitive decisions

1. **Title authority** — the two direct scan-46 lines control. `பாட்டரங்கில்` is rendered **At the Revolutionary Poet's Poetry Gathering** and `தலைமைக் கவிதை` as **Presiding Poem**. No date, venue or birthday wording is promoted into the title.
2. **`குடி` opening wordplay** — English retains the token *kudi* because the joke moves between the lexical drinking/habitation sense and the suffix in place names. Karaikudi, Kannangudi, Kundrakkudi, Kumarakudi, Mangudi, Manakkudi, Lalgudi, Aykudi, Pothakkudi, Surakkudi and Kallakkudi remain visible in sequence. The scan-46/47 contrast `கல்லக்குடி` → `கள்ளக்குடி` is represented as **Kallakkudi** versus italic **kalla-kudi**, rather than flattening both into one English word.
3. **Source-form `பருக்கிடம்`** — scan 47 is final-cleared with the visibly printed unusual form `பருக்கிடம்`. English therefore retains italic **parukkidam** instead of guessing a person, institution or modern spelling not supplied by the source.
4. **Source-form `“சாரம்”`** — the verified correction from provisional `சாராய` to printed `சாரம்` is respected as **"Saaram" lodge**. Translation does not silently turn it back into “liquor.”
5. **Puduvai / proper-name forms** — **Puduvai**, **Pazhaiya Perumal**, **Puduvai Sivanurai**, **M. Po. Si.**, **Avvai Shanmugam** and other source-visible names are retained conservatively rather than normalized from outside biographical knowledge.
6. **Place/semantic sound play** — `நஞ்சை / நஞ்சை`, Puduvai/new-intoxication phrasing and the repeated `சிறப்பு` sequence are kept as explicit lexical repetitions where English can carry them; Tamil tokens are retained where the English pun would otherwise disappear.
7. **Bharathi / Bharathidasan naming play** — the translation preserves **Bharathi**, **Bharathidasan**, `பார் அதி` as source-visible *paar ati theeran*, **Kanaka Subbu Rathinam**, and the “servant of Bharathi” explanation instead of replacing the passage with an external biographical paraphrase.
8. **`கச்சிதம் / கச்சு இதம்` and `ரசவாதம் / அதிரசவாதம்`** — the sound play is kept through *kacchu*, *rasavatham* and *athirasavatham*, with enough English context to make the sash/alchemy/sweetness movements readable. No claim is made that English reproduces the Tamil phonetic effect exactly.
9. **Embedded Bharathi/Bharathidasan quotations** — all quoted verse is translated directly from this final-cleared Tamil witness. No published external English translation was imported. Source political and literary claims remain source claims.
10. **Scan 50 unusual `விணுக்குறியா?`** — the final-cleared source explicitly preserves this unusual form. English retains it as italic **Viṇukkuṟiyā?** rather than inventing a semantic repair unsupported by the canonical source.
