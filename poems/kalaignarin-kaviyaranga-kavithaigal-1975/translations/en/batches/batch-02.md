## Source-sensitive decisions

1. **`மை` architecture** — the central sequence `தொன்மை / எளிமை / வண்மை / வலிமை / இறைமை / புதுமை` is rendered with readable English glosses plus the Tamil-derived `-mai` forms (*thonmai, elimmai, vanmai, valimai, iraimai, pudhumai*). The later `ரஷ்ய மை / ரசாயன மை / உண்மை / இம்மை / மறுமை` chain likewise keeps *mai* visible so the joke remains recoverable.
2. **`மாதவர் / மாதொரு பாகன்`** — the opening pun is not flattened into a single theological paraphrase. The translation retains *madhavar* / *mādh-oru-bhāgan* and identifies Lord Siva only where the source itself does.
3. **`அன்பிலார்`** — retained as *Anbilar* where the source supports a name/form pun, while the immediate “without love for me” turn is translated separately.
4. **`கிறுக்கு` repetition** — retained through italic *kirukku* around writing/scribbling/madness rather than replacing the repeated sound with unrelated English words.
5. **Kundrakkudi / calf-house play** — the Chennai `கன்றுக்குடி` and `குன்றக்குடி` sound relationship is preserved by keeping the institutional Kundrakkudi name visible and not modernizing it away.
6. **Murugan–Valli episode** — Valli the woman and `வள்ளிக்கிழங்கு` the edible tuber are kept linked as **Valli / valli-kizhangu**; the joke is not replaced by an outside myth retelling.
7. **Pari–Kapilar narrative** — the daughters, Three Crowned Kings, Kapilar's duty, north-facing death, and friendship satire remain in the source's sequence and are translated as source claims.
8. **Political satire** — Ettappars, election defeat, “Russian mai / chemical mai,” and the truth/this-world/next-world wordplay remain explicit; the translation does not neutralize the political joke.
9. **Poet introductions** — Ilanchera poet, Savarlal, Muthu Sambandam, Ayyakkannu, Singara Vadivelan, and Mudiyarasan remain source-visible names/forms without outside harmonization.
10. **Closing programmatic sequence** — antiquity, simplicity, generosity/violence, strength, divinity and novelty are kept tied to the preceding `mai` structure through scan 65; no prose summary replaces the rhetorical conclusion.
11. **Structural boundary** — all eight physical scan markers are retained exactly once, and the source horizontal rule after scan 65 remains a non-lexical comment.
