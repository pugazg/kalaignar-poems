## Voice / fidelity rules

1. Keep direct address direct.
2. Keep rhetorical questions as questions.
3. Preserve repetition and parallelism where they carry rhythm, humour or argument.
4. Preserve public-oratory energy and conversational pivots; do not prose-summary them.
5. Preserve satire, accusation, ideological contrast and self-deprecating humour.
6. Preserve embedded quotations and attributions distinctly from Kalaignar's own voice.
7. Translate quoted Bharathi/Bharathidasan lines from the final-cleared Tamil witness; do not silently import a published English translation.
8. Source political/historical claims remain source claims; translation is not a fact-check layer.
9. English may adjust line breaks for grammar but must not erase poetic/rhetorical structure.
10. Keep dates, numerals, initials and source-visible institutional references traceable; do not silently expand an initialism absent from source.
11. Preserve scan provenance with `<!-- scan_page: N -->` markers in English item files.
12. Represent source closing rules structurally, not as invented lexical text.
13. If English exposes a genuine Tamil discrepancy, formally reopen the Tamil workflow before continuing.

- Item 02 names and literary/historical identities — Pari, Kapilar, Murugan, Valli, Avvaiyar, the Three Crowned Kings and Kunnakudi/Adigalar context — must remain source-traceable and not be harmonized to an outside retelling.
- Item 04 `சுதந்திராக்கள்` is source-sensitive; do not flatten it to generic “free people”. Batch 03 must document the final English handling from the source context.

## Batch workflow

For each batch:

1. fetch live `main` and preserve newer durable work;
2. read the assigned final-cleared canonical item completely;
3. consult `SOURCE_MAP.md`, Phase-3 title/join records and relevant verified page records where source-sensitive wording requires it;
4. draft only the assigned English item while retaining scan markers;
5. record title/term/wordplay decisions explicitly;
6. compare source coverage scan by scan for omission, duplication and accidental prose compression;
7. review voice, quotation/speaker boundaries, names, dates, source claims and rhetorical structure;
8. create/update `batches/batch-NN.md` as the durable decision/review record;
9. mark a batch `reviewed — PASS` only after its complete item passes.
