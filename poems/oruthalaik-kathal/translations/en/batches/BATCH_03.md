- Batch-01 title lock **One-Sided Love** is retained unchanged.
- Section 5 preserves the mother/daughter argument as an argument, not a prose summary. The mother’s social/class warning, Porkodi’s counter-argument, childbirth imagery and river-depth analogy remain distinct rhetorical movements.

- `பொருள் விளக்கம்` remains **Source explanation**.
- Section 6 preserves the victory procession’s paired imagery — streets as flowers, sky as banners — and the contrast between returning warriors and households receiving a garlanded sword as indirect news of death.
- The recurring martial lock is retained in both scan 49 and scan 54 contexts: **war-sword / flower-petal**. Scan 49’s broader vow also retains the explicit love of the Tamil land and the source’s battlefield/death imagery without softening.
