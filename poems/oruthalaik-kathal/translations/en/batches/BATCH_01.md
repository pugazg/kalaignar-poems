## Title decision

The English work title is fixed as **One-Sided Love** for the Phase-4 translation layer.

Reason: `ஒருதலைக் காதல்` literally and repeatedly frames the work as love flowing from one side. “One-Sided Love” preserves that lexical and rhetorical shape more closely than silently replacing the title with the more interpretive idiom “Unrequited Love.” The Tamil title remains present in metadata and remains the canonical source title.

## Fidelity decisions

- Direct address, rhetorical questions, repeated emphasis and dramatic speaker turns are retained rather than compressed into prose summary.
- Proper names are kept in readable source-traceable forms: **Nakkannai, Perunarkilli / Narkilli, Sattanthaiyar / Sattanthai, Tittan, Porkodi, Perungozhinaykan, Kavarpentu**. Source-specific honorific forms such as **Poravaik Ko-Peru-Narkilli / Poravaikko Perunarkilli** are not silently harmonized away.
- Sangam source quotations remain visibly distinct quotations with their source work, poem number, poet and praised hero where the Tamil gives them.
- The *Purananuru* 82 English rendering is informed by the source's own immediately following prose explanation; no outside-edition wording or emendation is imported.
- The *Purananuru* 85 quotation remains separate from Nakkannai's own following simple-language explanation; the two layers are not collapsed.
- Source `பொருள் விளக்கம்` blocks are translated separately as **Source explanation** rather than merged into the narrative.
- The culturally loaded sequence `அச்சம், நாணம், மடம், பயிர்ப்பு` is retained in transliteration and immediately glossed as “fear, modesty, reserve and decorum” instead of erasing the Tamil formulation.
- The source's polemical language about sacrifice, atheism, heaven and Tamil tradition is translated as source rhetoric; it is not fact-checked, softened or editorially reframed.
- Source bodily/romantic imagery, including the closing description of Nakkannai on scan 20, is retained rather than euphemized away.
- Illustration scans **8** and **16** are represented only by neutral structural markers, because the source supplies no lexical body caption.
- Section-closing `♦     ♦     ♦` witnesses on scans **13** and **20** are retained.
