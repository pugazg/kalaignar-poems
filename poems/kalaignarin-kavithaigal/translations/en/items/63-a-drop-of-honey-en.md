# A Drop of Honey!

<!-- scan 425 -->

Sibling,

> “They heed not hunger, sleep not with their eyes;
> for them, their task itself is the eye.”

That is an old song—
it will be engraved in your mind!
To see a new turning,
to tear away the disguise of the “holy,”
to make wicked deeds fall,
we have donned the garb of battle!
This election is one stage of it!
Money is being poured out by the basket;
decorations leap and dance;
coloured lights steal the eye!
With black money they will cover even “the pond”!
But you, apple of my eye, do not worry—
take the Kazhagam flag in your hand!
Wear the Rising Sun upon your clothes!

<!-- scan 426 -->

Go street by street!
Go house by house gathering votes!

★

Between one another,
among yourselves,
let there not be even one drop of bitterness—
for that tiny drop
can become a great flood!

★

Why are you waiting?
Whom are you waiting for?
Even if someone comes,
will he not give only a drop of honey?
Think that it has not come,
and bend your body
into a bow!

★

In the tents of those
who have looted honey and milk
by the pot, variety upon variety,
heap upon heap,
you plead with your eyes, my dear!
Where is such a pot here?
There is only the “crown”
of being Anna’s younger brother!

★

To heal wounded Tamil Nadu,
to end the cruelty of those
arrogant in ruling the land,
to create a new condition—
O storm, rise!
Rise at once and act
like a tiger cub!

★

<!-- scan 427 -->

The days remaining are few;
fly like a weapon, my dear!
Is victory a commodity sold in a shop?
Without slackening your stride,
hurry around the constituency!
Shake off weariness! Show your strength!
With not even time
to wipe away your sweat,
O life of the Kazhagam
that has spread like a banyan—
fly like a spear
and seize victory!

## Translator note

The `★` divisions are source structure and remain explicit. “The pond” is kept in quotation marks because the Tamil itself marks `குளத்தையே` that way; no outside referent is supplied. The poem's honey/drop, bitterness/drop and pot imagery is intentionally recurrent and is not flattened.
