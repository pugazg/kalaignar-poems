# Anna Is Here

<!-- scan 227 -->

Extracts from the presiding poetic address by
Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi
at the *kaviyarangam* held in Kalaivanar Arangam, Chennai,
on 2 September 1972.

Sandalwood!
Young breeze that comes gliding from southern Podhigai!
Unfailing lamp!
New flower of fragrance!
Great one who taught us
to guard as we guard our eyes
the virtues called dignity,
duty and discipline!

Muthamil itself,
which captured every thought
of lion-hearted Tamils
and now, beside the Bay of Bengal,
with lips closed and eyes closed,
rests in silent penance!

Leader who created a good government
so that every hut might rise
and shine like a temple tower;
so that the darkness of ignorance
and murderous poverty might perish and retreat;
so that everyone might attain every welfare
and live in joy!

<!-- scan 228 -->

Anna of the younger brothers
who understand your thought before it is spoken
and bring it to fulfilment!
My very life,
who made me what I am
and protected me here!

Day after day
we carry out the noble work
of making every one of your dreams real.
In every task we perform,
Anna — you are here!

Mother!
Tree that called me “younger brother”
in a mother's voice!
Whenever my mother calls me,
I hear you calling.
In my mother's call,
Anna — you are here!

The poor are smiling
under the government of your younger brother.
Many say they see God in that smile.
But we —
we see you.
In the smile of the poor,
Anna — you are here!

One day in Arur,
an old woman who had regained her sight
called your younger brother “God.”
And that “God”

<!-- scan 229 -->

raised his hands
and worshipped you there.

Yes, Anna!
When the knife-edge opens
the eyes of those poor people,
in those very eyes —
in that recovered vision —
your smiling face appears, ayya!

Morning and evening,
while we labour without rest,
at every moment,
at every hour,
in everything,
Anna — you are here!

And more than that...
when, after finishing our work,
one day we come
to sleep in your lap,
Anna —
you will be there too.

Our Anna who sleeps
on the wave-washed seashore!
Unquenchable lamp!
Light of love!
Bearing the flame of the lamp you gave us —
the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam —
we shall drive away
all the deceitful acts of the men of darkness
and raise the flag of victory!

## Translator notes

- `அண்ணன் இருக்கின்றார்` is rendered **Anna Is Here**, preserving the poem's repeated claim of Anna's continuing presence rather than treating the title as a purely locative statement.
- The repeated refrain `அண்ணனே! நீ இருக்கின்றாய்!` is kept as **Anna — you are here!** to preserve its direct-address cadence.
- The references to welfare measures, restored eyesight and the DMK lamp are translated as the poem's political-elegiac rhetoric, without external commentary.
