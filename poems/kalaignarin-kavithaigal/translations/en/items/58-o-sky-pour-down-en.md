# O Sky, Pour Down!

<!-- scan 415 -->

*When the sky failed and drought spread across Tamil Nadu, Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi wrote this in* Murasoli *on 23 March 1975, pleading for rain.*

The forest-fire of drought—
put it out with the tears
you distil!
Where is your flashing smile?
Where is your thundering speech?
For so many long days,
without your rose-water bath,
my precious Tamil land
has yearned—understand that!
O sky, pour down,
to wipe away our people’s torment—
upon the martial Tamil people
who hold honour greater than all else.
Some at the Centre think
that by fastening stains upon us

<!-- scan 416 -->

and hurling
the knife of slander,
they can snatch away the crown—
pour down, O sky,
to sharpen their wits!
Hindus, Muslims,
and the Christians who live here—
our kin—are pleading:
“Like ancient Tamil’s three rains,
let it pour!”
Good people call you;
the ungrateful wish
you would show no mercy—
stretch out your water-hand
so their burning hearts may cool!
O sky, pour down—
that the Tamil world may live!

## Translator note

The poem joins drought imagery to contemporary political accusation. `மும்மாரி` is rendered as the classical “three rains” rather than replaced with a generic “abundant rain”; claims about the Centre remain source rhetoric.
