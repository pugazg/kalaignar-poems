# You Bless Your Footwear

<!-- scan 461 -->

Today is your servant of love's
fifty-seventh birthday!

Scholars say that looking back
at the path already walked,
and not straying from the path now being walked,
is the gaze of a lion.
This humble one is no lion;
yet, keeping the road already travelled in mind,
I shall continue my service to the Tamil people!

Koḷili is the town that sowed me—
sweet Tamil also calls it Thirukkuvalai!
Raised there like a seedling,
I learnt the breeze-like Tamil of Thiruvarur!
At the school of Erode,
I received education in the dignity of our people;
at Kanchi, with knowledge as charioteer,
an education in ideals!

To oppose Hindi imposition forever,
in the Tamil army that first set out,
I saw the youth wing even then
and became inseparably joined
to the movement's course!

From that day until today:
Ayya Periyar's embrace!
And, enfolding me,

<!-- scan 462 -->

⟦continuation from previous page⟧

the moist tenderness of Anna's heart!
Beloved sibling,
what thought do I have
other than you?

Even when I lay upon the railway track,
even when solitary prison roasted me,
the tears shed by the Anna who saw me
were rosewater sprinkled
to wash away my weariness!

When, after I had crossed twenty years of age,
they tormented me in Pondy,
the medicine Father Periyar rubbed on me
was an ambrosial feast
that cooled my mind!

My mother Anjugam
and Muthuvel,
when they gave birth to me,
waited thinking:
“He will sing music
and make us happy!”
But this one
was caught fast
in the midst of a politics
that sings abuse!

An ideal is not jasmine
that one can simply reach and pluck!
Nor is it a mango or coconut
to climb and pluck!
It is swimming against the current!
Planting a flag upon a volcano!

Government that appears along the way
is only shade offered by nature
so that the fatigue arising on the journey
may pass.
The journey toward the ideal
must continue!

<!-- scan 463 -->

⟦continuation from previous page⟧

Birds of a pond whose good water has dried
may act like kith and kin,
then seek the office to which they had clung
and sell themselves.
Even so,
when a crowd that vows,
“We shall always be arrows in the bow;
we shall live as spear-points,”
keeps its word with firmness
and flashes like letters carved in stone,
why should I worry?
The Kazhagam itself is our east!

They say a horse threw its rider down
and then dug a pit for him.
Here, those who made us the horse
and rode upon us
have come to dig a pit for us!
The story of kicking away
the ladder one climbed!
The story of killing gratitude!

All these torments
that have become stories—
apple of my eye, sibling,
I endured them
by thinking of you!

You bless me!
I bow to you!
What you bless is not a crown!
Whether a crown exists or not,
you are the sibling
whose nature is to bless.

For you,
you bless this footwear
that keeps labouring!

<!-- scan 464 -->

⟦continuation from previous page⟧

For this alone
you polish it!
You are the poor, humble sibling!
Even when this footwear wears thin,
you will not say,
“Stop now and rest!”

On this birthday, sibling,
I give you my pledge:
never leaving you,
I shall labour without rest!

*(Written by Kalaignar as his message for his 58th birthday.)*

## Translator note

The source body opens by calling the day the speaker's `57`th birthday, while the source's closing parenthetical identifies the text as Kalaignar's message for his `58`th birthday; both witnesses are preserved exactly rather than reconciled away. `காலணி` is deliberately retained as “footwear”: the closing conceit rejects the crown and casts the political worker as footwear worn down in service to the sibling. Place-names and movement-history references follow the final-cleared Tamil witness without importing outside biographical wording.
