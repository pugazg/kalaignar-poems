# When Does Defeat Come?

<!-- scan 408 -->

Because a little mouse has come,
a mighty cat hides
beside the stove.
At the sight of a snake fleeing,
a mongoose stands trembling,
drenched in sweat.
If such enormous spectacles,
unseen anywhere in the world,
should arrive in rich-Tamil land,
then every lion-like Tamil bull—
long crowned with victory,
never weary of a fight—
will hide like a fox
frightened by a crab
and hand victories
to foreign okra-warriors!

## Source publication note

Published in **1945**.

## Translator note

The deliberately comic animal reversals and the source insult `வெண்டைக்காய் வீரர்` are kept literally enough to preserve the satire; “okra-warriors” is not an outside historical label.
