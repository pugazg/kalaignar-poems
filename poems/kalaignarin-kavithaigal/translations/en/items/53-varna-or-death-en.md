# Varna or Death?

<!-- scan 407 -->

In the green Tamil land
that lived with grace,
singing many a *parani*,
a few selfish tricksters,
without even a grain of honour,
may gather and devise
scheme after scheme,
trying somehow
to establish *varna*;
but at death’s very summit,
to guard your honour—
O martial Tamil,
fight!

## Source publication note

Published in **1945**.

## Translator note

`வருணம்` is retained as *varna* because the poem's political force depends on the caste-order term and its sound opposition to `மரணம்` / death.
