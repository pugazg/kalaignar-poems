# Know It as a Storm!

<!-- scan 405 -->

Eripathar slew his enemies
with the battle-axe!
Mother, father,
wife and children were hacked down
by Kotpuli!
And you call Saivism
the ground on which love stands!
In the killing-field,
Jains died through the prison of caste!
You self-styled true devotees,
raving in swollen pride!
The mythology you nurtured
to snare us in its net—
we shall smash it against the charnel ground
and bring it down in an instant!
We are not a breeze
entering a garden of flowers!
Know us as a storm!

## Source publication note

Published in **1945**.

## Translator note

The poem invokes violent episodes and sectarian polemic from Saivite hagiographic tradition as Kalaignar's source argument. The English translates that rhetoric as written rather than reconciling it with devotional accounts.
