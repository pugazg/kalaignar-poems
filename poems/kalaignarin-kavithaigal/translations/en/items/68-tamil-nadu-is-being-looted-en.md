# Tamil Nadu Is Being Looted

<!-- scan 438 -->

Tamil Nadu is being looted, mother,
by the torment of corruption-bandicoots!
Tamil Nadu is being looted, mother—
by the cruelty of a ruinous government;
in life, even daytime turns dark with grief!
By the torment of corruption-bandicoots,
Tamil Nadu is being looted, mother!

Did she not climb to power
being called the Rani of Jhansi?
Yet she bought the government's TANSI land
and broke the law!
She dances at the summit of arrogance—
and now,
to wipe out that conceit,
the people are speaking!
By the torment of corruption-bandicoots,
Tamil Nadu is being looted, mother!

They say there is corruption of six crores in Omni buses;
they sell the government beer factory
and pocket two crores.
Brother, if we do not wake even now,
they will turn our country into a cremation ground
and stretch prison bars across it!
By the torment of corruption-bandicoots,
Tamil Nadu is being looted, mother!

<!-- scan 439 -->

With a rowdy force they run an entire realm;
newspapers that criticise them
are burned in the street.
Government property is sold without tender;
many bribes are taken underground!
By the torment of corruption-bandicoots,
Tamil Nadu is being looted, mother!

For revenge alone, they say, “Supreme-Court appeal,”
and run all the long way to Delhi;
but then they say a High-Court verdict is enough
and hand a medical college over to private hands!
By the torment of corruption-bandicoots,
Tamil Nadu is being looted, mother!

TAMIN, SPIC-share corruption, they say;
rice-mill corruption,
teacher-training-school corruption, they say;
lavish-tour, wasteful-expense corruption, they say—
can all of it even be told today!
By the torment of corruption-bandicoots,
Tamil Nadu is being looted, mother!

## Translator note

`பெருச்சாளி` is a large rat/bandicoot and functions here as the poem's repeated metaphor for corrupt operators, so the refrain keeps “corruption-bandicoots.” TANSI, Omni bus, the government beer factory, TAMIN/SPIC, courts and the other allegations are translated strictly as claims made by the poem; Phase 4 does not independently adjudicate them.
