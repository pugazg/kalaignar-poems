# We Move as Your Shadow!

<!-- scan 379 -->

Written on the seventy-fifth anniversary remembrance of Anna.

★

Anna!
Our very life!
Our feeling!
King of our people's dignity!
Form of uprising!
Lovely grove
where thoughts bloom!
Heartbeat of your dear younger brothers,
bars of steel!

Seventy-five is born for you — yet
why did the count end at sixty?

Anna!
In beautiful Tamil speech
you surpassed waterfalls!
And that rain of English words —
a ceremonial fan before your power!
You heaped up sugar-candy letters like a mountain;
we turned into ants
and tasted them!

<!-- scan 380 -->

Seventy-five is born for you — yet
why did the count end at sixty?

Anna!
Magnetism in your eyes!
Calm in your speech!
Valour in your action!
Wisdom in your method!
Affection was your sibling;
compassion, its reprint!
A heart that could bear anything!
Sacred treasure found by the Tamils!

Seventy-five is born for you — yet
why did the count end at sixty?

Anna!
Before you came,
Tamils had a history — but
only after you came
did the Tamil understand that history in its true form!
Tamils had literature!
Yet only after you came
did you set it in motion
so this world would know it!
The Tamil people were a race with a distinct culture;
at a time when the Tamil people themselves did not know it —

<!-- scan 381 -->

Leader! Because you arose,
the Tamil became Tamil!

Seventy-five is born for you — yet
why did the count end at sixty?

Anna!
You were Father Periyar's commander!
The many kinds of harm
wrought by strange men —
you called them a potful of poison!
You were a scholar!
A teacher of knowledge!
You declared that nurturing the class division
that defines one as poor and another as rich
was evil,
and spoke standing on the path of equality!

Seventy-five is born for you — yet
why did the count end at sixty?

For this question that rises,
no answer alone has been found!
The grief within us
has not subsided even on this day.
Yet in the thought
that you have never gone away,
we move as your shadow!

## Translator notes

- The canonical title carries a terminal `!`; the contents witness does not. The English title follows the canonical witness.
- The repeated “seventy-five / sixty” question is retained as the poem's structural refrain rather than reconciled to outside biographical age-counting.
- `ஆலவட்டம்` is rendered as a ceremonial fan, preserving the image of English verbal fluency attending Anna's larger oratorical power.
- `மொந்தை விஷம்` is kept as the source's deliberately coarse “potful of poison” image; no outside textual variant is substituted.
