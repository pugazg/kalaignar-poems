# Would They Accept?

<!-- scan 404 -->

Dharman and Bhima
and the other three were not enough —
Panchali loved Karna too.

Ah!
Lakshmi and Parvati,
and Kalaimagal, lady of music,
did not dry the ocean up
because chastity had been lost!

Ahalya,
woman of a rishi lineage,
joined herself to Indra;
Tara,
the ascetic woman,
desired the Moon.

All these Puranas
the pandits of this land
fashioned and gave us
in Muttamil —
because their spines were bent!

Women who speak the heroic words,
“Even if a mountain comes against us,
we shall not let our chastity crumble” —
if all the women named above are gods,
would people with self-respect
accept what you say?

## Source note

Published in **1945**, according to the verified source page record.

## Translator notes

- The mythological and sexual claims are translated as Kalaignar's source polemic, not harmonised with outside epic/Puranic traditions.
- `முத்தமிழ்` is retained as **Muttamil**, the threefold Tamil conception, because a generic “Tamil” would lose the source's literary-cultural charge.
- The unusual `கடலைதனை வறுக்கவில்லை / கற்பிழந்த காரணத்தால்` construction is kept close to the source rather than supplied with an explanatory mythology absent from the Tamil.
