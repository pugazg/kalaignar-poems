# Have You Heard?

<!-- scan 406 -->

Have you heard?

“Landlord of three hundred,
four hundred *veli* of land—
has even one poor person
risen because of you?”
My comrade of this land,
who asks this at every hour!
I welcome and honour
the question from your stout heart;
I grow into a tree
the moment your question rises!
A thousand *veli* of land
belong to stone in the temple, friend!
Yet not even a blade of grass
is of use to the poor,
who go on lamenting!
Through clenched teeth, one word—
O hero of Bharata,
have you ever heard it?

## Source publication note

Published in **1945**.

## Translator note

The canonical title `கேட்டுண்டோ?` is deliberately retained as distinct from the source-body closing `கேட்டதுண்டோ?`; the Tamil archival layer is not normalised. *Veli* is retained as the historical land-measure term.
