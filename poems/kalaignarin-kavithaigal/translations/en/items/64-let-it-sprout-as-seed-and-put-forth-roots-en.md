# Let It Sprout as Seed and Put Forth Roots!

<!-- scan 428 -->

Let it sprout as seed
and put forth roots!
In the womb of Mother Paddy-field,
made pregnant by the farmer’s sweat,
she came as a daughter—
the maiden Grain-ear,
cherished by everyone;
on Pongal day she stands before us!
Like a woman in the bridal chamber,
her head bowed,
those called Tamils rejoice;
they leap crying *thai, thai!*
With light’s arrival darkness disappears;
chisel-carved sculptures glitter!
On Pongal day,
so that new joy may appear everywhere,
the Sun has risen!
To approach everything with civility
and overcome it,
the Rising Sun has climbed high today!
Let us change the condition
that asks,
“Has the life of the Tamil people
become only a story?”—
let our feeling of ethnic self-respect
sprout as seed
and put forth banyan roots!

## Translator note

`விழுதுகள்` evokes the prop/aerial roots of a spreading banyan, not merely generic roots; the closing rendering makes that image explicit. `உதயசூரியன்` retains both the natural Rising Sun and its movement/political resonance. The contents title's `விட்டோம்` is not merged with the canonical imperative `விடட்டும்!`.
