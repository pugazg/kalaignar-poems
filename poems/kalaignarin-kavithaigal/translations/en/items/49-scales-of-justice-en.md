# The Scales of Justice

<!-- scan 403 -->

You will put medicine on a wound
and seek a way to make it heal!

You will understand
that a widow too has a heart within her,
like the pupil within the eye!

“My wife has died;
I want another marriage,”
you will say!

Then, if you yourself
reach the edge of death,
must your wife sacrifice
her love as an offering?

## Source note

Published in **1945**, according to the verified source page record.

## Translator notes

- The source lineation splits `கைம்பெண்` (“widow”) across lines as `கைம் / பெண்`; the English restores the lexical unit only at translation level and does not alter the Tamil source layer.
- The final question is retained as a direct challenge to the gendered double standard around remarriage rather than softened into explanatory prose.
