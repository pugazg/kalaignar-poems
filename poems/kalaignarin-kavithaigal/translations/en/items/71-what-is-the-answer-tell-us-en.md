# What Is the Answer? Tell Us!

<!-- scan 446 -->

Among all lands, a good land—
our flourishing Tamil land!
But scalding water was poured at its roots,
and ancient culture burned and collapsed!

Within that town stands a poisonous tree—
like those who run to eat its fruit,
thinking it has ripened for food,
and lose their lives,
the people cast their votes
and brought ruin upon themselves!

It is easy for some
to put on the costume of the virtuous;
it is hard to recognise it
and remain awake!

The rule of those who once said
“ten-and-a-half-touch pure”
now bears witness by sweeping away
all the wealth there is!

“Everything to private hands”—
they pour the country away
and go to welcome ruin without restraint!
Hereafter there is no life here for the poor;
they have even blown the conch
to clear the way for outsiders!

Young people,
rising with a shiver like leopards,
like lion cubs!
Brothers who turn into
an erupting volcano, a storm,
and make history!

<!-- scan 447 -->

Shall we say, “Enough of endurance,”
and rise in a surge?
Or shall we become donkeys
whose sense has died,
and carry still more loads?

## Translator note

`பத்தரை மாற்று` is the same old gold-purity register encountered earlier in the anthology; “ten-and-a-half-touch pure” keeps the source metaphor rather than replacing it with a generic claim of honesty. The privatisation and electoral accusations are translated as source claims.
