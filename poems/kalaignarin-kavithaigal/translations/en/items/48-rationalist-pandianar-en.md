# Rationalist Pandianar!

<!-- scan 400 -->

Our dear Pandianar!
Was *soundaram* — beauty — only in your body?
Your heart too was beautiful!
Firmness itself was your tiger-like strength;
your bearing had a grandeur like Panneerselvam's —
so Anna Alagiri would often say
of Pattiveeranpatti Pandianar;
I too was among those
who savoured those words from close by.

I, Anbil and Nagore Hanifa
rejoiced in the hospitality of Pattiveeranpatti,
a feast where honey and milk seemed blended.
We ate and delighted
in fruits of rationalism.

The next morning,
you arranged for us to be shown and taught
all about the fragrant cardamom,
vegetables and flowers
in the hillside estate;
it remains in memory even now!

P. T. Rajan, who recreated Ayyappan,
and Soundarapandian, who said
“*Poyyappa kadavul*” — “false, father, is god” —
lived as friends,
their hearts one within the other
like ghee within butter —
what a quality that was!

One day at Arur,
at a meeting we young Dravidians conducted,
you took part;
the speech you gave there
was worth a thousand pieces of gold!

<!-- scan 401 -->

“In the name of the Self-Respect Movement,
whatever Periyar says,
I shall accept as command;
let us hold the coral-jubilee conference
in ancient Madurai,”
you said!

That one event conducted by the DMK
under the name **Pandian Nagar**
is proof that this is a movement
that does not forget to turn back
and look at history!

When a splendid bridge was built
at a cost of eighty lakh
in Cheranmahadevi,
the constituency of Pandian,
who had served as Assembly Speaker,
I gave it the name
of sweet-Tamil Soundarapandian.

To the tooth-baring crowd
that fixes its principles
on whichever side comes to power,
stands worshipfully enslaved to the ruling party,
your words would have delivered
thousands upon thousands of whip-lashes,
Pandianar!

Those Mahamagam men
who chant the Hari-name mantra,
smearing it over as “evolutionary development,”
now unveil the handsome sacred form
of the commander of the rationalist movement
who lived like a lion —
what “appropriateness” indeed!

At the Chengai Self-Respect Conference
that Ayya conducted in 1929,
the far-seeing resolution
that women too must have equal rights in property
was adopted under your leadership;
remembering that,
we duly made it law
under the DMK government.

Not only Nadars —
the people of the land themselves
praise you and honour you.

<!-- scan 402 -->

Only when caste is abolished
can society advance —
never for even an atom of time forgetting
the Tirukkural of Valluvar,
the primal son;
O joint of sweet sugarcane!
O unwavering lion-bull!
May you live forever!

## Translator notes

- `சவுந்தரம் / சவுந்தர்யம்` is allowed to carry both the name-sound and the ordinary sense “beauty”; the English exposes that wordplay rather than replacing it with a flat adjective.
- `அய்யப்பனை ... / பொய்யப்பா கடவுள்` is a source polemical wordplay. `Poyyappa kadavul` is retained before a compact gloss so the sound-play with Ayyappan is not erased.
- The poem's claims about personalities, conferences, statutes and political conduct are translated as source claims; this English layer is not a historical fact-check.
