# Still This Clamour?

<!-- scan 409 -->

Still this clamour?

On the banks of the Cauvery,
in the surge of the Pennai—
there is no history! No history!
Alas, Tamil Nadu;
they say the beginning is in the Ganges!
Who has seen that the Pandyas ruled?
Who carved the Cheras’ prosperity?
Has anyone told of Chola abundance?
The tale of the sandal’s rule alone
is made the beginning of history!
All who reject it, they say,
have sold their honour.
Students have come to reshape it!
Cadre-warriors will flash forward!
O slack-jawed ones—
still this clamour?
