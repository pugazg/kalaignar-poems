# Today Is Your Birthday

<!-- scan 394 -->

Written by Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi
on Kamaraj's birthday, 15.7.76.

Today is your birthday —
a festival everywhere
for that mountain of principle!
Born in Virudhai,
you grew into a warrior
and stood as a leader
who filled history!

The cruelty of prison,
torture itself —
you accepted them
with a smiling face,
a man of sacrifice.
We had a thousand
clashes of opinion — yet
shall we ever forget
your imperishable service?

Sacred one who came
to make Tamil soil fragrant!
Embodiment of public service,
sweet as nectar!
Thought dwells through your very being — and in the heart,
for as long as thought remains,
it remains there to labour!

<!-- scan 395 -->

Khadi clothed your body;
your arms embraced
the crying poor.
You would launch harsh words
at political parties —
yet if hardship came
to a party leader,
you would tremble with concern!

Your tears
at Periyar's grave!
Your tears too
when Perarignar and Mutharignar passed away!
And when I lost
the mother who gave me birth,
you came like kith and kin
and shed your tears!

Great man!
Did not our tears for you
flow like the swelling
of a mighty river?

Your life was not
the life of a private man;
it was a chapter
in the history of self-respect.
From Kumari to the Himalaya
you made your flag fly
and inscribed the fame
of splendid Tamil Nadu.

“Man of virtue!
Tender shoot of a noble lineage!” —
so our Anna,
bridegroom of culture,
called you.
The Father of Rationalism,
calling you a *pachai Tamilan*,
a true Tamil,
affectionately kissed
the crown of your head.
Did not this sea-girt nation
fold its hands before you
as the “Black Gandhi”?

Toward the edge of your life
you showed affection toward us too!
You established your very life
as a lesson for everyone!

Today is your birthday;
forever it is a special day!

— Published in *Murasoli*, 15.7.76.

## Translator notes

- The canonical title uses `உன்றன்`; the contents witness uses `உன்`. Both mean “your” in this context, so the English title is the same while the Tamil lexical variant remains explicit in metadata.
- `விருதையில்` is retained conservatively as **Virudhai**, the source's place form, rather than silently harmonized to an outside modern place-name spelling.
- The verified source repeats the unusual form `கருத்திருக்கும்` in `கருத்திருக்கும் உன்றன் உடல் ... நெஞ்சில் / கருத்திருக்கும்...`. English preserves the repeated “thought” sense rather than silently emending the Tamil to a different-looking word or imposing an unsupported death/body interpretation.
- `பேரறிஞர்` and `மூதறிஞர்` are retained as the source's honorific identities **Perarignar** and **Mutharignar** rather than silently replacing them with outside names.
- `பச்சைத் தமிழன்` is retained once as *pachai Tamilan* with an immediate contextual gloss, and `கருப்புக் காந்தி` is translated directly as “Black Gandhi,” preserving the poem's source epithets.
