# A Letter in Verse!

<!-- scan 417 -->

Sibling! I shall tell you one thing—
keep it in mind!
We are indebted for all time
to guard the country.
Those who stand in contradiction
have no work in this Kazhagam.
Those who lived by grabbing a place
will go somewhere else;
brothers who do not leave the track
will stand as one.
Whatever quarter of Indian soil
may send danger,
at once we shall raise
shoulders of valour
like the Vindhyas and Himalayas
and overcome it.
If harm comes to the country,
the DMK will spring up—
setting aside that very instant
its own resolution and demand,
never forgetting to beat its chest
to guard the honour of the soil.
This is the witness of our Kazhagam’s history;
I am not saying something new today.
Our duty, always, is service to the country.
Let us guard peace
so the people may live well;
let us make a way
for good principles to endure.

## Translator note

`கழகம்` is retained as Kazhagam where the poem speaks in movement-family language; `தி.மு.க.` is rendered as DMK. The national-duty claims are translated as the source's political self-description.
