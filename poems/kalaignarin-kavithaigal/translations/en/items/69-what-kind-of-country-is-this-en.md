# What Kind of Country Is This?

<!-- scan 440 -->

What kind of country is this?
What kind of country is this—
a fraud that gives life only to cheats!
Affection and attachment here are like water and oil!
Ah, friendship that sheds false tears
and then cuts your throat!

Every heap of rubbish imagines itself a sharp sword;
with drums and war-drums pounding,
it dances in swagger!
When those who came to rule strike through plunder,
the crowd of servants beating time grows fat!
To stretch out a hand and receive money and office,
they stand baring every tooth in their mouths!
With bags full of bribe-money,
lakhs upon lakhs poured out,
if told, “Put your head at my feet,”
they will place it there without hesitation!

What kind of country is this—
a fraud that gives life only to cheats!
There is no boundary to the sky in this world;
there is no boundary to poverty in India!
Ten scandals come and go in a year;
the Harshad Mehta scandal is a badge of disgrace!

“We stand firm in policy,” they say;
“We stand for conduct,” they say;
“The flag and symbol belong to us,” they say!
Then they beat their chests for wages
in return for the money they were given;
until the giving hand closes,
they are scorpions that keep jumping!

<!-- scan 441 -->

The dominant classes have come to power;
harm may come in many forms,
yet the press applauds!
What kind of country is this—
a fraud that gives life only to cheats!

“I alone am the heir to Periyar and Anna,” one says.
If a fox asks us to believe it is a lion,
people still say, “Long live the name,
for this one rules the land!”
“We prospered because of that person;
we shall be subjects to none,” they say.

Once, men would not live
if even a kunrimani-weight of honour were shed;
today, even after a mountain of honour is lost,
men as great as mountains bend
without shrinking at all!
Has the mountain sunk and the ridge risen—
is that story now a serial without end?

The three-letter word called gratitude
is turned into filth and eaten;
the land has become a sewer
where pigs too wallow!
What kind of country is this—
a fraud that gives life only to cheats!

A child for whom a mother fasted first,
carried three hundred days in her womb,
now hurls a spear to make that belly collapse!
The DMK is the beautiful moon
that flowered after driving away dusk;
because it lies beyond the monkey's reach,
the monkey howls until the street laughs!

To resist Hindi,
to guard our people and language,
this fortress stands;
there is a plan to destroy it!
“I am always first for the feast,
you are always first for the battle”—
the clinking small coins run about
thinking and saying just that!
Like Gandharis who ask Kunti for a child
and stab their own bellies—such is their desire!

<!-- scan 442 -->

What kind of country is this—
a fraud that gives life only to cheats!

If it is the leader's birthday,
by all means let a garland be placed!
But at every shrine,
ministers carrying kavadi—
is that the work of rationalism?
They shave their heads;
they leap crying “thaththom”;
they roll wearing leaves;
women too walk through fire!

All the rays of knowledge
that the Self-Respect Movement
and the cry of dignity for our people
caused to grow in Tamil Nadu—
have they all stumbled and fallen
because the servant-mind climbed into the head?
When will a new awakening rise in Tamil soil?

Do not hesitate—set out, Tamil!
Slap your broad shoulders
and rise!

## Translator note

The poem's repeated `எத்தன்` points to a cheat/trickster, hence the refrain “a fraud that gives life only to cheats.” Its political, corruption, ritual and mythological accusations are preserved as source rhetoric. The Kunti/Gandhari passage is intentionally satirical and is not harmonised with outside epic versions.
