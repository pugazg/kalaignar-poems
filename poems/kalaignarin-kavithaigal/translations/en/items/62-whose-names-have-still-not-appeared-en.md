# Whose Names Have Still Not Appeared?

<!-- scan 422 -->

Whose names,
whose names have still not appeared?
Sibling,
only by receiving the funds
I ask you for as a right
have we steered the Kazhagam boat
across fierce storms!
Whenever funds were asked,
you were like generous Pari,
never saying no;
even when you had nothing,
you were like Kumanan of Kongu,
ready to say,
“My head is still here—take it!”
Beloved apple of my eye,
that is how you shone then
and how you shine today!
Perhaps Muthuvel and Anjugam
knew it beforehand
when they named me *Nidhi*—fund—
so Perarignar once said
before the Mutharignar,
and received eleven lakhs.
When I ran about and came seeking you,
you searched me out
and brought the fund!
Do you remember
that event whose green freshness
has never faded from the heart?

<!-- scan 423 -->

The Kazhagam treasury has not become pregnant—
what can be done?
Family planning
where it is not needed!
Some treasuries swell and rejoice,
not through lovers’ quarrel and union,
but through corruption!
Those are no infants—
Central Investigation doctors say
they are great abdominal tumours.
From that day to this,
to serve the rare aim
of taking only in measure
and living in prosperity,
the Kazhagam has remained
a treasury unstained!
The five and ten in your hand
at once become Kazhagam election funds
and place you before me.
The moment I see your name,
I bless you from my heart!
Nor do I fail to count
whose names have still not flashed
in *Murasoli*.
One word for tomorrow—
you know me!
If it came whirling
with the sweep of a sword,
would that be pleasant for you?
Even if I merely ask in sweet speech,
“What did this person give?”
would it not strike like thunder?
So today itself, sibling,
without making excuses,
send the Kazhagam fund at once!

<!-- scan 424 -->

Periyakulam must be crossed by swimming—
with your precious labour.
Give the Kazhagam fund!
When this Karunanidhi asks,
O jewel of the Kazhagam,
have you ever kept your fist closed? Hmm!
Before an eye can close and open,
heap it up—
like a hill!

## Translator note

The poem repeatedly plays on `நிதி` as “fund” and as the final element of Karunanidhi's name; English exposes that wordplay instead of inventing an equivalent. The treasury/pregnancy, family-planning and corruption-tumour imagery is intentionally comic and political. Named exempla and monetary claims remain source claims.
