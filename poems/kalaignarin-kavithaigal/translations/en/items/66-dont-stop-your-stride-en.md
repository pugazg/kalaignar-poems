# Don't Stop Your Stride!

<!-- scan 433 -->

Sibling,

dogs will go on barking.
Do not stop your stride for that!
There is no need to tremble, shrink,
or move aside to the edge.
We are not going to loot, after all;
we are going to stop the looting!
To spread the principle—to reach the goal—
to climb the hill of victory and plant the flag!

The seeds of discord scattered by enemies
will grow into thorns.
Pure-hearted one, they will pierce your feet;
so that they do not wound the feet
of those who follow you,
lift them out,
break their points,
and fling them far away!

Do not panic at the wild torrent
that cuts across the path.
Reckon how your grandfather, great-grandfather,
mother and father crossed it;
build a bridge,
or show the strength of your shoulders and swim—
reach the bank without letting the journey
be stopped halfway!

One pack of howling foxes will go to the right;
another will go to the left.
What if they go right? What if they go left?
Only see that they do not leap upon you
and tear at you!

Beasts and great pythons will writhe in the bushes—
only to clear away such dangers
do you need a weapon in your hand!
To destroy human enmity
that heaps itself like chaff,

<!-- scan 434 -->

carry the sword called civility
and the shield called love!

When, with resolve, you climb
peaks covered in frozen snow,
footwear without inner chafing will serve you well—
it will never, ever slip!

Your journey is to remove
the filthy thoughts and burdens of domination
piled up for thousands upon thousands of years.
Do not forget that purpose
and leave the Tamil a refugee!

Enemies will appear as friends:
they will sing a prelude in your praise,
show you office,
even bear your palanquin.
Guard yourself from falling into that pit,
and guard the people too.
Beat the drum and sing, “Long live Ayya Periyar!”
Sound the conch and dance, “Long live Arignar Anna!”
Then every hard journey can continue without fatigue.
Even the tiny ant scorns those who live without labour!

Know your duty;
flower in dignity and win.
Let the Kazhagam journey go on
without discipline breaking down!
Vow that there is not even a moment's rest.
Sibling, rain upon Tamil Nadu
so that prosperity may spread!

If you must, throw a little food now and then
to the rats and termites along your path;
but take care not to feed certain newspapers!

The Kazhagam is a crop of a hundred thousand seasons.
Watch out for the tricksters
who offer honey mixed with poison
to uproot it completely!
For the principle joined to our flesh and life,
we shall conduct a procession of victory!

## Translator note

The poem sustains one political-journey metaphor from barking dogs and thorns through torrents, snow peaks and treacherous hospitality. `உட்பூசலற்ற காலணி` carries both the physical sense of footwear without inner chafing and the movement sense of freedom from internal feud; the English keeps the footwear image and leaves the political resonance audible. `கடமை / கண்ணியம் / கட்டுப்பாடு` remains the movement triad “duty / dignity / discipline.”
