## Translation review

### Item 46 — There Is No One Day Called His Birthday!

Preserved:

- Anna as the southern Tamil land's `இதயவேந்தன்`, the king of hearts;
- jaggery, blossoming-flower, word-savour and writing-fragrance imagery;
- poisonous-fanged snake / heart-as-snake-charmer's-flute conceit;
- daily `அர்ச்சனை` remembrance, tortoise-like restraint and mute endurance;
- the sudden fire image when harm reaches Tamils;
- the brooding-hen protection image across the scan-396→397 join;
- the final pearl-word / diamond-eye images and the paradox that every day nectar-Tamil is born is Anna's birthday.

The source-visible syntax `அடைகாக்கும் கோழி போல / அவனைத்தான் நம்மையெல்லாம்-` remains untouched in Tamil; English preserves the broken rhetorical join rather than silently substituting a presumed Tamil repair.

### Item 47 — Precious Remedy! Anbazhaga, Beloved Sibling!

Preserved:

- the sixtieth-jubilee / family-festival frame;
- Tamil-fragrance-on-the-tongue, Kazhagam-garden and long-sleep awakening imagery;
- new-flood speech, race-breath and victory-sword parallelism;
- `அகம் / புறம்` retained as *akam / puram* literary categories;
- the poet gathering and Professor's sharp-spear rebuttal image;
- Periyar's children / Perarignar's brothers / inseparable two-coloured flag movement lineage;
- the final long-life blessing.

Canonical `அன்பழக` is treated as the direct name-address **Anbazhaga** with its affection resonance; the contents witness `அன்புறவு` remains a separate source variant.
