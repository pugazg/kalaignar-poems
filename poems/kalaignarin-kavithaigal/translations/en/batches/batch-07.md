- independence-lamp and blood-as-oil imagery;
- warning against allowing destructive forces into “our country / our people / our government”;
- the complete named sequence: Bharati, V. O. C., V. V. S. Aiyar, Subramania Siva, Champakaraman Pillai, Vanchinathan and Tiruppur Kumaran;

Preserved:

- Periyar/Anna movement split and “double-barrelled gun” image;

- all eight guest-poet Anna roles and the closing “smile of the poor” return;
- non-contiguous item provenance: **230–236, 238**, with no scan 237 inserted.
