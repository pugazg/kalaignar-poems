## Translator notes

1. **Title witness** — canonical `இதயத்தைத் தந்திடு அண்ணா` controls **Give Me Your Heart, Anna**. The contents witness `இதயத்தைத் தந்திடு அண்ணா!` is retained in metadata and is not merged into the displayed title.
2. **Anna** — `அண்ணா` is retained as **Anna**, preserving the political, personal and movement-specific form of address rather than flattening it to “elder brother.” `பேரறிஞர் அண்ணா` in the context note is retained as **Perarignar Anna**.
3. **Structural scans 18–19** — scan 18 is the title witness and scan 19 is marker-only/structural. Both remain in English scan provenance even though scan 19 contributes no edition text.
4. **Flower/body imagery** — `உள்ளம்`, eyes, hands and feet repeatedly become flowers. The English keeps the deliberately accumulative flower imagery rather than replacing it with generic praise.
5. **Parani/classical passage** — *Kalingathu Parani* and the `parani` form remain explicit. Source-visible `கழற்சென்னி` is rendered conservatively as **Kazhar-chēnni** rather than silently identified or normalised from an outside edition.
6. **Tamil “three letters” sequence** — the rhetorical chain `வாழ்வு`, `அன்பு`, `அறிவு`, `காதல்`, `வீரம்`, `களம்`, `வெற்றி`, `அண்ணா` depends on Tamil orthographic counting. The Tamil tokens are therefore retained beside readable English glosses; English spelling is not falsely presented as reproducing the three-letter effect.
7. **Anna quotations/headlines** — quoted slogans and editorial phrases remain direct quotations in the order and rhetorical force of the final-cleared Tamil. Phase 4 does not adjudicate their publication history.
8. **Movement vocabulary** — `கழகம்`, Dravidar Kazhagam and `முன்னேற்றக் கழகம்` remain movement-specific rather than being dissolved into generic “organisation/party” language. Where the source uses only the shortened `முன்னேற்றக் கழகம்`, English keeps **Munnetra Kazhagam** rather than silently expanding the Tamil wording.
9. **Historical/political claims** — the 1938 language agitation, independence-day disagreement, 1949 split, election/government chronology, World Tamil Conference and contemporary praise are translated as Kalaignar states them. Translation is not a fact-check layer.
10. **V. V. Giri wordplay** — the source moves from V. V. Giri to `கிரி` (“mountain”) and then to Anna as a cloud embracing the mountain. The English keeps the explicit “If Giri means mountain” pivot so the source pun remains traceable.
11. **Source-visible Kural-like anomaly** — scan 28/final-cleared Tamil reads `அழுக்காறு அவர் வெகுளி - இன்னாச் சொல்...`. English retains **`avar`** as a source-form token instead of silently changing Tamil to the familiar `அவா` reading. Any source-backed correction would require reopening the Tamil archival layer first.
12. **`கடிதோச்சி மெல்ல எறிய` allusion** — the rod/staff passage is translated from the final-cleared wording and left as Kalaignar's allusive rhetoric; it is not replaced by an outside quotation.
13. **Statue sequence** — Avvai, Kannagi, Bharathi, Bharathidasan, Veeramamunivar, Caldwell, Pope, Kambar, the “Tamil who steered the ship,” and Tiruvalluvar remain source-visible; the unnamed source phrase is not silently replaced by an externally normalised personal name.
14. **Sea-shore elegy** — the final sequence preserves the shore, pearl, sea-breeze, buried body, heart and flower-feet imagery as direct address. It is not softened into detached memorial prose.
15. **Printer's ornament** — the centered closing ornament is retained as a structural note after the translated verse.
