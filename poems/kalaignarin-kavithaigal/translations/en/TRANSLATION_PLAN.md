## Governing objective

> Retain Kalaignar's language — voice, cadence, rhetorical architecture, political and historical specificity, literary allusion, repetition, satire, dialogue and direct address — while making the English genuinely readable.

Readability is not permission to paraphrase away source texture, fact-check a source claim inside the translation, modernise an argument, flatten repetition, or replace rhetoric with translator explanation.

## Translation source hierarchy

Normal working source: final-cleared canonical `../../sections/NN.md`.

If a Tamil reading must be questioned:

1. controlling source scan — ultimate textual authority;
2. verified `../../pages/NNNN.md` page record;
3. final-cleared canonical `../../sections/NN.md` working text;
4. Phase-3 audits/reviews governing pagination, joins, title witnesses, assembly and final clearance.

A translation difficulty is never authority to edit Tamil silently.

## Stable item and title policy

- Tamil canonical identities remain **1–77** and English retains the same stable item number.
- Canonical Tamil `title` controls the displayed English title translation.
- `contents_title` remains a separate witness in English metadata.
- Source-valid punctuation/wording variants are not merged into a hybrid title.
- Pure anthology group-divider witnesses remain collection structure, not poem items.

## Voice-fidelity rules

1. Direct address remains direct.
2. Repetition and parallelism are meaning; do not compress them for elegance.
3. Public-speech and elegiac oratory must remain audible.
4. Rhetorical questions remain questions.
5. Political/historical names, dates, movements, institutions and claims remain explicit.
6. Literary/classical references remain traceable rather than generic.
7. Kalaignar's imagery is retained even where literal English is unusual.
8. Satire, accusation, ideological contrast and polemic are not neutralised.
9. Quotations and speaker changes remain distinguishable.
10. Historical subject matter does not justify fake archaism.
11. Source notes, separators and structural markers remain visible where relevant.
12. English may adjust line breaks for grammar, but must not proseify the poem or erase stanza/rhetorical architecture.
13. Source claims are translated as source claims; Phase 4 is not a fact-check layer.
14. If translation exposes a genuine Tamil discrepancy, reopen the Tamil source-backed workflow before proceeding.

## Names, terms and wordplay

- Keep established proper names recognisable while respecting the Tamil witness.
- Preserve culturally active Tamil terms when translation would erase identity.
- Where a source wordplay depends on Tamil orthography, retain the Tamil form/transliteration and explain the loss or choice in a separated translator note.
- Do not silently substitute an outside historical spelling or textual variant for a final-cleared source form.

## Batch cadence

Complete-item boundaries are mandatory. A batch must never cut a poem merely to meet a target size.

Batch size is adaptive to source length and complexity:

- long/complex items may form a one-item batch;
- routine work may combine two or three complete adjacent items;
- source-order interposition remains authoritative and must be represented rather than reordered.

## Batch workflow

For each batch:

1. fetch live `main` and preserve any newer durable state;
2. read assigned final-cleared canonical item files completely;
3. consult `SOURCE_MAP.md` and Phase-3 title/provenance records;
4. consult verified page records for source-sensitive wording, notes, quotations or joins as needed;
5. draft English without changing Tamil;
6. review source coverage and omissions/duplications item by item;
7. review Kalaignar-language/voice fidelity;
8. verify names, dates, figures, literary works, movement vocabulary and speaker distinctions;
9. record non-literal/transliteration/wordplay decisions in translator notes;
10. mark the batch `reviewed — PASS` only after all assigned items pass.

Only reviewed item translations will be eligible for full English collection assembly.
