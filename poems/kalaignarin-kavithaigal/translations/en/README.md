## Translation principles

- retain Kalaignar's voice, cadence, direct address, repetition, imagery, satire/polemic and rhetorical questions;
- retain political, historical and literary specificity rather than generalising it away;
- translate source claims as claims, not as corrected history;
- preserve canonical/contents title-witness distinctions in metadata;
- preserve source notes, quoted speech, internal structural breaks, illustrations/ornaments and scan provenance;
- never repair or modernise Tamil silently from the English layer;
- if a genuine Tamil discrepancy is discovered, reopen the affected Tamil archival layers before continuing translation.
