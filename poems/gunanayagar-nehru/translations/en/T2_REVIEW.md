## Tamil-first review

Primary authority: `../../sections/01.md`, blob `56ec1cf593d20c69f50a7b4bb7b69529bcc413e6`, with provenance markers **3, 4, 5, 6, 7 exactly once**.

The ten T1 review items were resolved as follows:

1. **Title** — `குணநாயகர் நேரு` is fixed for Phase 4 as **Nehru, the Noble Leader**. This preserves `குண` as moral/noble quality and `நாயகர்` as leader without borrowing the historical witness title.
2. **`பூமானே` / `கோமானே`** — retained as paired royal addresses, **O king of the earth!** / **O sovereign!**, without collapsing them into one epithet.
3. **`காலக் குரங்கின் கை ‘மாலை’`** — T1's mistaken **‘evening’** reading was corrected. The reviewed English now preserves the source's monkey/garland image: the monkey-hand of Time makes Rose's life a **‘garland’ in its grasp**.
4. **`மடி பறித்துச் ...`** — the awkward T1 `snatched freedom from our fold` was revised to a cautious source-faithful rendering describing the white rulers as having **torn into our fold and seized our freedom**; no unsupported historical expansion was added.
5. **`நேர்` / `நேரு` wordplay** — English cannot reproduce the Tamil sound-chain without inventing meaning. The reviewed text preserves its semantic steps — standing **straight**, asking what will **happen**, and becoming **Nehru** — while the batch record documents the lost phonetic play.
6. **`பிஞ்சும் பூவும்`** — rendered as **the tender flower-bud of a child**, retaining the source's child/flower image in the Uncle Nehru passage.
7. **`சீனக் கள்ளி` / `விடி வெள்ளி` / `பகை கிள்ளி` chain** — re-lineated into complete English sense-units: the army/foe/firebrand/black-mark/Chinese-thorn warnings, the **morning star**, and Nehru **plucking out and flinging away the foe**. Tamil rhyme is documented as non-transferable rather than replaced with invented English rhyme.
8. ***Kalingattu Parani* quotation** — compact `மணியதரத் தேதேனும்` is retained contextually as **Will no honey ever come to those jewel-like lips?**; the surrounding mother/war-field imagery was checked against Tamil and kept visibly quoted.
9. **Puduvai-poet stanza** — retained as a distinct quotation: foremost virtue, true/right justice, equality as democracy's substance, and the command to beat the drum and proclaim it. No attribution absent from the source was invented.
10. **Closing wordplay** — the reviewed cadence keeps **democracy / autocracy / rule of money / Noble Leader** structurally visible and revises the final syntax so `உடன் முனைந்திடக்` reads as Nehru **pressing forward too**, not an unsupported `with us`.

Additional Tamil-first corrections included removing the added sense **At last!** from `அதோ, அதோ!`, tightening the white-ruler passage, and preserving the certified scan **6→7** continuation without smoothing away its structure.

- the witness ends before the Tamil poem's Anna, China-border, *Kalingattu Parani*, Ashoka, Puduvai-poet and final democracy/autocracy/money-rule passages; the repository translation correctly retains all canonical Tamil material.
