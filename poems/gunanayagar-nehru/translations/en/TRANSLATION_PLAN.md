Rules:

7. Because the witness is selective/condensed and does not represent the whole Tamil poem line-for-line, absence from the witness is not evidence for omission from our translation.

## Translation fidelity controls

1. Preserve direct speech and speaker shifts among narrator/flowers/Rose.
2. Preserve rhetorical questions, repetition, ellipses and emotional escalation rather than flattening them into prose summary.
3. Preserve the poem's wordplay around `நேரு`, `நேருவானார்`, and `நேரு மாமா`; English may need documented adaptive rendering rather than false literal repetition.
4. Preserve distinct flower identities. Do not collapse `மல்லிகை` and `முல்லை` into one undifferentiated English flower without a deliberate note/decision.
5. Working flower controls: `மல்லிகை` → **jasmine**; `செண்பகம்` → **champak**; `சாமந்தி` → **chrysanthemum / samanthi** pending line-level fit; `முல்லை` → **Mullai** unless a clearer distinction-preserving rendering is established; `ரோஜா` → **Rose**.
6. Verified `கர்த்தபம்` means **donkey** and must not be normalized away.
7. `நேரு மாமா` → **Uncle Nehru** unless line-level review supports a better equivalent.
8. `கலிங்கத்துப் பரணி` should remain traceable as ***Kalingattu Parani*** rather than being silently generalized.
9. The quotation `யாதும் ஊரே யாவரும் கேளிர்` must remain visibly a quotation; its exact English rendering is a T1/T2 review decision.
10. The later quoted stanza beginning `தலையாகிய அறமே புரி` must remain visually distinct as quoted material; do not add an attribution the source does not print there.
11. Source claims are translated as source claims; Phase 4 is not a historical fact-check layer.
12. English lineation may adjust for grammar but must preserve the poem's rhetorical/poetic architecture and scan provenance.
