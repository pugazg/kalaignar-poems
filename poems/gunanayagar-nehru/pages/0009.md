# Scan 9 — printed English translation, part 2

## Source-supplied English text

```text
I grieved over Rose’s Tale
And returned,
Not severing any flower
From the tossing bower
But if I gather flowers
I will weave a wreath
And garland Nehru
A gloried Maha Meru?

He is a Hero
Who sculptured Freedom for Ind!

Bearing crucial lashes
He walked the Path
Gandhiji trod,
His youth spent
In prison den!

Though fuming in ire when irked
Flames of Sacrifice he nurtured
And born with golden means
He lived with men in Indigence.

Touring Ind
        Like a whirlwind—
        Thunder in words
        Manna in smiles

        Wisdom in pen
        Explosion in deeds
        Stubbornness in creeds
        Liberty in his veins

He fought with fists
All Injustices
And stood as fortress
When in distress,
Giving Indian mass
His cheering solace.

Can anguish accost you
If pure you live?
        —He asked as child
He was grand Uncle
        To millions:
        God and Religion
        He respected
        And reigned Ind —
        No one pained!
        He banned obsequies
        When leaving all the world
        And nobly Remained
        An Apostle of Peace—
        All country mine
        All kith and kin!

He never stood
As unapproachable!
Even when peaked in Power
He Remained—
        A sweet Salem Mango!
        A chosen Pearl!
        A silken Garb!

        A leaping Kaveri!
        A Tanjore Temple Tower!
        A revered Jawahar!

Translation: Dr. Krishna Srinivas
```
