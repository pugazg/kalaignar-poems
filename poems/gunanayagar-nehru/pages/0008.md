# Scan 8 — printed English translation, part 1

## Printed heading

```text
BEAUTY ROSE WEPT
```

## Source-supplied English text

```text
I will narrate
The Scene I saw:
In flower grove
I now entered:

Champak flower whispers
Stange tidings
Into the ears
Of Jasmine flower:

They both neared
A Samanthi flower
And whispered words:
Then all the three
Took Jasmine on ride
And walked the grove:

What do they seek
These flower women?
In search of thread
To weave a garland?

        I stood staggered!
“My foot aches”
        —Jasmine cried.
“My legs bleed”
        —Champack groaned

The flowers heard a wail
And rushed to that spot

‘O Lord! Renowned!
Leader Immaculate!
        Where is the end
        That left us orphaned?
        You kissed
        And took us to your heart,
        You honeyed our lips

And rhapsodised our hearts —
Drowning all
In depths of joy—
How dare you leave
We four bereaved?”

Hurling wails,
She shed
A flood of tears
And stood -
        An enchanting Rose!

Jasmine asked:
        “Why do you weep
        O Rose! So sween?
        That Goodman lived
        To save oppressed
        But now he is gone
        Stop your wail
        You, Queen Flower! Smile.”

But Rose still wept:
“Dewdrops were manna to me!
He will not wear me anymore!
Thorn pricks? Why this red?
Why this perfume?

He was on Throne of Bharath
And I throned in his heart!
A sleep will wake up:
I was to dance with him!
But he is gone!
My life a grim bygone!
Leave me alone and let me
On his birthday offer myself
At his tender feet!”
```
