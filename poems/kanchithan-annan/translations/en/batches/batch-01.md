## Source-sensitive decisions

1. **Direct title authority** — scan-16 canonical `காஞ்சிதான் அண்ணன்` controls **Kanchi Is Anna**. The catalogue description is not imported into the title. The emphatic/identity force of `தான்` is carried through the title and the later reciprocal “Kanchi is Anna / Anna is Kanchi” construction rather than by adding explanatory title text.
2. **`காஞ்சி` identity play** — English retains **Kanchi** as the same visible term for the periodical, the city and the final Anna/Kanchi identification. The translation does not flatten those occurrences into separate unrelated labels.
3. **`படைக்கலம்` / `நிலக்களன்`** — rendered “the weapon Anna left us” and “an arena for his feelings,” preserving the martial/ground imagery rather than modernizing the Tamil.
4. **Weekly repetition** — `வாராவாரம்` / `வாரம் முழுதும்` remains “week after week / the whole week,” retaining the source repetition.
5. **Golden-letter / golden-issue parallelism** — `பொன்னெழுத்து / பொன்னேடு` remains a paired “golden letter / golden issue” construction.
6. **`ஆற்றொழுக்கை` image** — rendered through “a river’s flow,” preserving the source flow/river comparison while keeping the line readable in English.
7. **Rationalist-procession image** — `பகுத்தறிவுப் பேரணிக்கு பாதை அமைத்த நடை` remains a style that “laid the road / for the procession of rationalism”; the political-rhetorical image is not softened.
8. **Laughter / thought pairing** — the two-line `ஒன்றைப்... / மற்றொன்றைப்...` contrast remains visibly parallel: “Read one... / Read another...”.
9. **Quoted `தம்பி`** — retained as **“Thambi”** rather than silently replacing the recurring source address with a generic English kinship term. Its contextual sense can be handled later only if a reader-facing note is needed.
10. **Grandeur / tenderness question** — the source question remains a question and preserves the paired `கம்பீரம் / கனிவு` contrast.
11. **Reciprocal identity** — `‘காஞ்சி’ தான் அண்ணன்...... / அண்ணன் தான் ‘காஞ்சி’......!` remains **“Kanchi” is Anna...... / Anna is “Kanchi”......!** with the source ellipsis and final exclamation state retained.
12. **`பொங்கல் மலர்` wordplay** — `Malar` is retained in *Pongal Malar* so the source’s publication-special / flower relationship remains recoverable; the following phrase is translated “a flower like gold.”
13. **First-person close** — `காத்திருப்பவர்களில் நானும் ஒருவன்!` remains explicitly first-person: **“I too am one among those who wait!”**
14. **Provenance and exclusions** — the English item contains exactly one `scan_page: 16` marker. Decorative title art, ornaments, design signature and portrait remain non-lexical source material and are not invented into the English poem body.
