## Governing objective

> **Retain Kalaignar's language — voice, cadence, rhetorical architecture, imagery, political-cultural memory and emotional force — while making the English genuinely readable.**

Readability is not permission to paraphrase away source texture.

## Voice-fidelity rules

1. Direct address stays direct.
2. Repetition is meaning and must not be compressed away.
3. Martial/public cadence must remain audible.
4. Family grief must not be sentimentalized beyond the source.
5. Honour, land, battlefield and kinship vocabulary must retain their source force.
6. Literary references such as `பரணி` require explicit, source-aware handling rather than generic substitution.
7. Images remain Kalaignar's images.
8. Source oddities are not translator licence to silently correct Tamil.
9. No fake archaism.
10. Translation choices that materially interpret culturally specific words must be documented.
