4. Preserve all eight poet-handoff headings as separate structural units.
5. Preserve all eight source `மு. க.` markers consistently as **M. K.** in the reviewed English.

8. Proper names, publication names, drama titles and literary references remain traceable to the Tamil source.
9. Source claims and political characterizations are translated as source claims; Phase 4 is not a fact-check layer.
10. English lineation may adjust for intelligible grammar but must preserve the poem's rhetorical/performance architecture.
11. Preserve physical-source provenance with scan markers through reviewed and assembled English.
