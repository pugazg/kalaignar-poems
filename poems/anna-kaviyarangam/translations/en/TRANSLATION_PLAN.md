## Objective

Produce readable English that retains Kalaignar's public-performance voice, political rhetoric, wordplay, repetition, direct address, historical and literary references, affectionate humour, dramatic anecdote and the staged succession of eight poets without converting the work into explanatory prose.

## Fidelity rules

1. Preserve source meaning before stylistic smoothing.
2. Preserve rhetorical repetition, parallel structures, exclamations, questions, ellipses and abrupt turns where they carry the performance voice.
3. Preserve the distinction between the scan-119 contextual note and the poem itself.
4. Preserve all eight poet-handoff headings as separate structural units; do not merge them into surrounding verse.
5. Preserve all eight `மு. க.` source markers structurally; their English rendering must remain consistent across the work.
6. Keep title-like forms distinct when the Tamil source distinguishes them; never manufacture a hybrid title.
7. In particular, preserve the ஆனந்தம் distinction between body wording and the `‘தலைவர் அண்ணா’` heading, and the வேழவேந்தன் distinction among `அன்னையாம் அண்ணா`, `‘அண்ணா அன்னை’`, and `அன்னை அண்ணா`.
8. Proper names, publication names, drama titles and literary references must remain traceable to the Tamil source. Transliteration or conventional English forms may be used consistently, but source differences are not silently normalized.
9. Source claims and political characterizations are translated as source claims; Phase 4 is not a fact-check layer.
10. English lineation may adjust for intelligible grammar, but must preserve the poem's rhetorical and performance architecture and must not proseify long verse passages.
11. Preserve physical-source provenance at review time with scan markers or an equivalent source map.
12. Any suspected Tamil defect must trigger a documented source-backed reopen; do not repair only the English derivative.
