## Source-sensitive decisions

1. **Title authority** — direct canonical `அண்ணா கவியரங்கம்` remains **Anna Kaviyarangam**. `கவியரங்கம்` is not flattened into a synthetic English title; the source-context prose may describe it as a poetry gathering where useful.
2. **Opening source context** — the scan-119 contextual panel is translated as a separate `Source context` block and is not absorbed into the poem.
3. **River-name wordplay** — the opening Kaveri / Vaigai / Pennaiyar / Palar sequence remains visibly name-bearing. The English keeps “Place a hand! Vaigai!” and “milk-river, Palar” rather than replacing the source’s sound/river play with explanatory prose.
4. **Physical provenance** — exactly six English `scan_page` markers are retained in source order **119 → 120 → 121 → 122 → 123 → 124**. Scan 119 alone carries printed page **19**; scans 120–124 remain `null` provenance. Scans 118 and 125 are not imported.
5. **Political and literary claims** — source characterizations of Periyar, Anna, Tamil politics, Hindi, caste, religion, democracy and government are translated as claims voiced by the poem. Phase 4 does not fact-check or soften them.
6. **Valluvar / Kanchi sequence** — the Mother Tamil / Valluvar dialogue remains a dialogue, preserves the Kural-derived sceptre image and retains the poem’s reincarnation-like movement into Anna’s birth in Kanchi without adding external explanation.
7. **Kural allusions** — the speech passages are rendered for meaning while preserving their aphoristic/quoted force; no external citation or modernized Tamil wording is inserted into the English poem.
8. **Eight handoff headings** — all eight concepts remain distinct and in source order: **Leader Anna**, **Anna the Speaker**, **Anna the Writer**, **Anna the Philosopher**, **Anna, Guardian of the Mother Tongue**, **Mother Anna**, **Anna the Actor**, **Anna the Chief Minister**. They are not normalized to one template.
9. **`மு. க.` marker policy** — all eight source `மு. க.` markers are rendered consistently as **M. K.**, each immediately following its associated handoff heading.
10. **Anandam distinction** — body wording around `தலைவராம் அண்ணா` remains separate from the source handoff `‘தலைவர் அண்ணா’`; the English body says “Anna the Leader” while the heading is preserved as the distinct structural title **Leader Anna**.
11. **Vezhavendan distinction** — the three source states remain distinct rather than collapsed: body-theme `அன்னையாம் அண்ணா` → **Anna as Mother**; quoted `‘அண்ணா அன்னை’` → **Anna, the Mother**; handoff `அன்னை அண்ணா` → **Mother Anna**.
12. **Food / feast imagery** — appam, aval and millet-flour remain visible rather than being replaced with generic “food”; the recurring feast metaphor is retained through “Serve it, sir!”
13. **Kothamangalam Subbu / literary-world imagery** — the oil-press-bull, peacock, garland-of-writing and Tamil-page images are retained rather than paraphrased away.
14. **Tamilpithan political wordplay** — `பிறை சூடும்` is translated through the visible crescent image and the source parenthetical Muslim-party alliance; the translation does not add political context beyond the source.
15. **Drama-title chain** — *Or Iravu*, *Velaikkari*, *Kumari Kottam*, *Chandrodayam*, *Chandra Mohan* and *Needhi Devan Mayakkam* remain transliterated as source-traceable titles inside the sentence that chains them together. They are not silently replaced by free English title translations.
16. **Muthulingam anecdote** — the Sambasivam / Durairaj stage incident, Karunanidhi’s prolonged improvised mourning, Anna’s shoulder-pain joke and the transition back to politics remain distinct; the affectionate humour is not flattened into summary.
17. **`சழக்கரை` handling** — the source-final-cleared term is retained conservatively as **Sazhakkarai** rather than guessed, normalized or silently replaced with an unsupported referent.
18. **Anna slogans / political rhetoric** — “a heart that can bear anything,” “He who has a younger brother fears no army,” and “If this army is defeated, what army can win?” remain visibly rhetorical and are not converted into expository prose.
19. **Abdul Rahman handoff punctuation** — the final source handoff uniquely ends with a period; the English heading likewise retains terminal punctuation.
20. **Closing catalogue** — the final accumulation Leader / Philosopher / Actor / King of Drama / Man / Gem / Minister / Mother / language guardian / Politician remains cumulative, ending in the one-word address **Anna** and the source’s explicit blessing.
