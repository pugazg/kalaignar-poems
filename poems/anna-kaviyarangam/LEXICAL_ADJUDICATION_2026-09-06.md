## Purpose

This record documents the Phase-1 word-level reconciliation between:

1. the initial repository working transcription;
2. a user-supplied full comparison transcription;
3. direct high-pixel inspection of the controlling 1968 scans; and
4. the user's subsequent manual source verification of the few readings where the user disagreed with the first high-pixel judgement.

This was a **lexical adjudication only**. It does not certify punctuation, lineation, missing paragraphs, page joins or structural completeness. Pages remain non-verified and Phase 2 has not started.

## Historical-Tamil rule

The repository historical-glyph policy controlled the inspection. Character identity was resolved before Unicode transcription; visually misleading old forms were not copied by modern-shape resemblance. No OCR output or later edition was allowed to overrule the 1968 pixels.
