## English translation

<!-- source scan 147 -->

Like a fruit tree, one woman alone—two monkeys!
What could she do?
As the price of her chastity, she lost a hand! She lost her nose!
Muthunagai lost the teeth that poured out pearly laughter!
Alas, she lost a breast—yet
she did not lose the steadfastness of chastity!
Even if she lost her head, a Tamil woman who would guard her honour—
Ravana's younger sister!

★

He heard the ear-searing news
that his guileless sister had been made Surpanakha—
he looked as though a wound had opened in his eye.
The king of the earth who rules the seas—
is he not Muthunagai's elder brother?
Even if no wider bond of kinship existed, what of it?
Is she not a citizen of Lanka?
He rose for revenge—his eyes storing red
like the young morning sun...
Hearing that the king was coming,
the beautiful-eyed Sita Devi,

wanted a winsome deer
and sent her husband away!
“While your elder brother is gone, little brother,
are you thinking of casting a net for your sister-in-law?
Go after the elder one, you too—she drove him off,
and waited for the Southerner to come.
The warrior came like a chariot, fire flying from his eyes;
he saw Sita come to the street entrance,
pouring honeyed words:
‘The elder and the younger have both run off—
you, the man suited to me, have come—
I give myself to you, she said, and embraced him,
Sita Devi of the bangles,
like a spreading jasmine vine upon Ravana's broad shoulders!
You are the wife of the man
who brought harm to my sister, dearer than life!
Rather than give your body to crows, vultures,
and lustful bears—
I will not even allow a hand to touch you.
Board the ship, he said.

<!-- source scan 148 — narrative continues directly -->

The wife of a man born to a lineage
that had put honour aboard a ship—
saying that he should accept her body as a gift,
thinking that by leaning upon his strong chest
she would somehow gain a boon—
climbed aboard the vessel and stood
behind the lion of majestic gait,
like a she-jackal!

★

“He will take the captive Sita Devi,
seat her upon the throne and make her crowned queen;
if Sita bears a son,
he too will have a share in Lanka's sceptre;
a land for the Brahmins, like palmyra fruit—
if it comes without labour,
what harm if ten thousand Sitas are lost!”

But things did not happen as the Aryans had imagined.
The king of Lanka arranged a prison
for Sita, the sleight-of-hand trickster!
As for that crowd proclaiming “Veda” and “sacrifice”...
he said: break their rib-bones
as if chopping firewood, and come!

The victorious king had a brother, Vibhishana—
a parasite upon a vengai tree,
waiting to bring down his elder brother and rule.
Into the hand of a fifth-column force called Hanuman
he placed a firebrand and said,
destroy the great city of Lanka ....

Across southern Lanka—a forest of fire, a forest of corpses!
Through the cooperation of corrupt Vibhishana,
the foxes—the Aryans—found a way
for their clan to flourish!

The cruel one who, through the stratagems of darbha grass,
killed his elder brother Vali, the Tamil warrior.

## Translator review — Gate B decisions

1. **Voice and stance — PASS:** the English keeps the poem's openly partisan praise, accusation, insult and political-cultural framing without neutralizing or intensifying it.
2. **Gate A terminology — PASS:** Ravana, Sita/Sita Devi, Aryan/Aryans, Brahmins, Vibhishana and Hanuman remain consistent with the reviewed EN-01 baseline.
3. **`இருமந்தி` — ACCEPTED:** rendered **“two monkeys”** rather than softened into a neutral reference to Rama and Lakshmana.
4. **`சூதறியாச்` — ACCEPTED:** rendered **“guileless”**; this preserves the source contrast without adding explanatory prose.
5. **`படர் உறவு` — ACCEPTED:** rendered **“wider bond of kinship”** to keep the compressed kinship image visible without inventing a specific legal or genealogical category.
6. **`பாந்தமுள்ள` — ACCEPTED:** rendered **“winsome”** for the deer; no additional physical description is added.
7. **`தென்னவன்` — ACCEPTED:** rendered **“the Southerner”** as a compact title-like reference to Ravana in this passage rather than expanding it into a new royal title.
8. **Scan-147 quotation anomaly — PRESERVED:** the opening double quotation before the sister-in-law accusation is not given an invented closing mark, and the later opening single quotation before Sita's speech is likewise not silently repaired. This intentionally mirrors the canonical Tamil's unusual quotation structure.
9. **`பைந்தொடி` — CONSERVATIVE:** rendered **“Sita Devi of the bangles”** rather than adding an unsupported colour, age or beauty attribute.
10. **`கரம் படவும் விடமாட்டேன்` — CONSERVATIVE:** rendered **“I will not even allow a hand to touch you”** so the translation does not force an unsupported choice about whose hand the Tamil implies.
11. **Scan-147→148 continuity — PASS:** `Board the ship, he said.` flows directly into the next scan's description of Sita boarding; the physical scan boundary is retained only in the source comment.
12. **`மானத்தைக் கப்பலிலே ஏற்றிவிட்ட` — LITERAL IMAGE RETAINED:** rendered **“had put honour aboard a ship”** rather than replacing the source's taunting metaphor with explanatory prose.
13. **`பன நுங்கு` — LITERAL IMAGE RETAINED:** rendered **“palmyra fruit”**; the image is not explained inside the poem.
14. **`செப்படி வித்தைக்காரி` — ACCEPTED:** rendered **“sleight-of-hand trickster”**, preserving the narrator's accusation without strengthening it.
15. **`ஐந்தாம்படை` — ACCEPTED:** rendered **“fifth-column force”** for the source's political/military metaphor.
16. **`குள்ளநரி ஆரியர்கள்` — RHETORICAL FORCE RETAINED:** rendered **“the foxes—the Aryans”** rather than neutralizing the insult.
17. **`தர்ப்பைகளின் சூழ்ச்சியினால்` — DELIBERATELY LITERAL:** rendered **“through the stratagems of darbha grass”**. The compressed source image is left visible; no explanatory identity is invented in the verse.
18. **Separator structure — PASS:** the single `★` on scan 147 and the single `★` on scan 148 are retained.
19. **User-controlled scan 148 — PASS:** `நின்றாள்`, `செங் கோலில்`, `மோசம்`, `என்றான்`, `வீடணன்`, `தமையனாம்`, `தர்ப்பைகளின்`, `சூழ்ச்சியினால்` and the other reconciled lexical forms govern this translation; no older conflicting reading returns.
20. **EN-03 boundary — PASS:** EN-02 stops at the end of scan 148. Scan 149 is not translated in this activity.
