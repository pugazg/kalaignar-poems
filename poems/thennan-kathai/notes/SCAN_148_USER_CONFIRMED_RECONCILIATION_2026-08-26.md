## Failure pattern

This page reinforces the project-wide finding that degraded old-print scans can produce confident but incorrect visual word recognition. A reading that appears visually plausible is not sufficient when an exact corrective witness is supplied by the user.

For future reconciliation work:

1. do not defend a prior visual reading merely because it survived multiple passes;
2. when the user explicitly identifies an exact extraction as correct, use that supplied text as the lexical control for that page;
3. document the superseded reading rather than silently replacing it;
4. keep the work-wide final audit open until every page has been subjected to the same standard.
