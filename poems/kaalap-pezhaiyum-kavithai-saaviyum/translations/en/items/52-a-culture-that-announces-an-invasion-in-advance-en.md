# A Culture That Announces an Invasion in Advance!

<!-- scan 257 -->

A culture
that announces an invasion in advance!

“You keep circling and circling
only through Chola history;

we are those attached
to the history of the Pandyas.

Once the histories
of Pallavas and Cholas are complete,

come a little quickly
to the Pandya side” —

not merely those I favour,
nor merely those who do not favour me;

many dear to my heart
called upon me with that wish.

Among the old histories
I come carrying as verse,

I ask loving hearts
to read the poem I shaped
with the Pandyas placed first —

published as early as 1945,
when I was working
at Ayya's *Kudi Arasu* office.

In a moment
you will feel
the turbulence within me!

“Along Kaveri's bank,
        in Pennai's rushing flow,

there is no history,
        there is no history!
Alas, Tamil Nadu —

the beginning, they say,
        is at the Ganga!
Who saw the Pandyas rule?

Who carved the Cheras' prosperity?
Has anyone told
        the abundance of the Cholas?”

<!-- scan 258 -->

I wrote another poem too,
with still greater speed:

“Where is the glorious place
        where the Chera lived?
Why did the brave Pandyan's rule fall?

In the tireless land
        where the Cholas moved,
how did a mass of companionable ideals gather?

Questions exploded
        in a heart that burned......

O bulls possessed by zeal for the land!
Throw down the men seized by fear!

At the summit of the tiger's life
        let us engrave the new!”

How could one
who wrote poems like these
forget the Muttamil Pandya,
one among the Three Crowned Kings?

★ ★ ★

Where should Pandya history begin......
with whom should that history begin first?

Those who stood
as the root-substance
for the three Sangams to sprout,

for them to blossom
as First, Middle and Last,

and those who supplied
the substance of thought as well —

were the Pandya kings of old.

Among them,
the first, the source says,
was Mudathirumaran.

There is a list
of Pandya kings said
to have founded and conducted
the First Sangam,

from “Kaisinavazhuthi”
to “Kadungon”;

but the history offers
none of the evidence required.

<!-- scan 259 -->

Even in the *Iraiyanar Kalaviyal Urai*
on the Middle Sangam,

however hard one searches
in the light,

no details of the earlier rulers
who conducted it,
apart from Mudathirumaran,
seem to emerge.

What does shine clearly
is the news that Pandyan Mudathirumaran
founded the Last Sangam!

Historians say
that this was also the time
of the second sea-deluge,

when Kumari
and the Pahruli river
were utterly destroyed.

R. Raghava Iyengar says
Mudathirumaran himself
was the Pandyan Nilandaru Thiruvin.

“After him came
the Pandyan Mudukudumi Peruvazhuthi” —

so, the source says,
U. Ve. Sa. Aiyar records it.

Poet-lords
Karikizhar,
Nettimaiyar,
and Nedumpalliyathanar

wrote poems
praising this king.

Among them is this:

“When a king comes
with his army
to besiege and attack a country,

first, so that the innocent people
of that land may know,

he announces the army's coming
through the beat of the drum —

what culture
in the conduct of war!

And now —
where has that culture hidden?”

★ ★ ★

There is a history
like a purana
about the Golden-Handed Pandyan!

I shall praise it briefly;
understand it well!

Every day,
in a changed disguise,

and even at midnight,
the Pandyan king
would come to inspect the city.

One night,
he heard a Brahmin
saying farewell to his wife
before leaving town —

<!-- scan 260 -->

“The king is there
to guard all of us.”

A month passed.
The husband returned.

Standing by the door,
the king heard
husband and wife speaking inside.

Without realising
that it was the husband,
he thought some thief
had come,

and knocked upon the door.

Then, learning
that they were husband and wife,
the king hurried away.

In the Aiyar's house,
suspicion fell upon the Ammami!

“Who was that man
who knocked at the door?”
he asked,

and the husband
came to doubt severely
his wife's chastity.

The king worried:
“Has confusion entered
that Aiyar's house because of me?”

To remove the doubt,
he went about knocking
upon the doors
of every house in the town.

The next day,
the townspeople came
to the palace
and told the king
the story of a thief
who had knocked at doors
in the middle of the night.

The king asked:
“What punishment
shall we give that thief?”

The council of ministers said:
“Cut off the hand
of the thief who knocked.”

At that very moment,
the king raised his sword
and cut off his own hand —

then opened his heart
and told the assembly
what had happened.

<!-- scan 261 -->

For the severed arm,
the townspeople fashioned
a golden hand
and fitted it to the king.

From that day,
he won fame
as the Golden-Handed Pandyan.

They wrote the puranas
with at least some order;

it was the histories
they buried away
like treasure!

★ ★ ★

Among the Pandyas
of the Last Sangam age:

Arivudainambi;

Boothapandiyan,
the incomparable poet-king
in the line of the fine poets
who gave Ollaiyur;

Ilamperuvazhuthi,
who vanished into the sea;

Nedunchezhiyan,
who crossed the Aryan army;

Pandyan,
who won the battle of Talaiyalanganam;

and rulers such as
Nanmaran
and Vetri Vel Chezhiyan —

all have already
found places
in many of my portraits in writing.

So rather than expand again
upon all that Pandya history,

let us continue
with the history of the Pandyas
who created a golden age

during the Last Sangam period
and afterwards.

★ ★ ★

An epic ancient in time;

an epic that does not begin
with an invocation to God;

an intellectual treasury
presented in the noble assembly
of Pandyan Nilandaru Thiruvin,

before elders and learned men,

<!-- scan 262 -->

with the affectionate,
sweet blessings
of the teacher Athankottasan;

that rare work
raised high and praised
by scholars —

*Tolkappiyam*.

On the day
of its presentation,

I did not merely enter
that garden of epic learning;

I invited you too,
softly saying,
“Come —
this is not the heat of summer;

it is the coolness
of the Courtallam falls.”

So,
starting from Madurai
of the Golden-Lotus Tank,

I shall speak in a series
of the great warriors
who ruled the other regions
of Pandya country as well!
