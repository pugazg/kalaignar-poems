## Translation controls

- `ஆந்தையும் அரசனும்!` → **Andhai and the King!**; `Andhai` is retained as the personal-name referent used for Andhaiyar/Pisirandhaiyar, not translated as “owl”.
- `கோழியூர்` / `உறையூர்` → **Kozhiyur / Uraiyur**, preserving the poem's place-name wordplay rather than substituting a modern explanation into the verse.
- `கோப்பெருஞ்சோழன்` → **Kopperuncholan**.
- `பிசிராந்தையார்` / `ஆந்தையார்` → **Pisirandhaiyar / Andhaiyar**; title and closing references use **Andhai** where the poem does.
- source variation `புல்லாற்றூர் எயிற்றியனார்` vs scan-20 `புல்லலூர் எயிற்றியனார்` is retained as **Pullarrur Eyitriyanar** vs **Pullalur Eyitriyanar**; English does not silently regularize the Tamil witness.
- `புறப்பாட்டு` → **Puram songs**, preserving the literary-category term.
- `முப்பாலும்` → **the Three Parts**, deliberately avoiding an unsupported expansion of which three divisions the source intends.
- `வடக்கிருத்தல்` is rendered descriptively as **sitting facing north / northern fast**; the poem's own explanation remains the contextual authority.
- `பல கல்` at the late journey is rendered **many miles** as a distance sense; no numerical conversion is asserted.
- `பூவரசு` is retained as **Poovarasu** rather than assigning an unsupported person/tree interpretation in English.
