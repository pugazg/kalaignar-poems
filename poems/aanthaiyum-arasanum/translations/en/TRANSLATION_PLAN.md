## Title decision

Tamil canonical: `ஆந்தையும் அரசனும்!`.

English: **Andhai and the King!**

`ஆந்தை` is retained as **Andhai** in the title because within this poem it refers to the poet Andhaiyar/Pisirandhaiyar; translating it as the common-noun “owl” would distort the poem's named-person referent.
