- `கைசிவந்த` is conveyed as the giver's hand reddened through giving, preserving the generosity image.
- `கொடைய நீரூற்று` / verified `கொடை நீரூற்று` is rendered **spring of giving**.
- `தம்பியுடையான் படைக்கஞ்சான்` is rendered as the proverb-like thought **“He who has a younger brother fears no army.”**
- `ஆவிதரநேரிடினும் அருந்தமிழ்க்கே தருவேன்` is rendered as giving one's life only for precious Tamil; no outside historical gloss is added.
- `ரத்தமல்ல; செவ்வண்ணம்` is retained as **“It is not blood—it is red colour.”**, preserving the poem's revelation rather than rationalizing the device.
- the royal `குடை` at the close is rendered **royal parasol**.
