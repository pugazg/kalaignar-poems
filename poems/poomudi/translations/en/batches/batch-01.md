## Translation decisions checked

- `பூமுடி` → **Flower Crown**: preserves the central flower/crown image and the later `பூ முடியே ! புகழ்முடியே !` address;
- `அறிவுமணங் கமழுகின்ற` → **fragrant with the scent of wisdom**: retains the fragrance/wisdom compound;
- `மா, பலா, வாழை` → **mango, jackfruit, banana**; the unusual source full-stop/exclamation sequence is represented as two English clauses rather than silently dropping the rhetorical break;
- `விழிமலர்கள்`, `கால்மலர்கள்`, `கைமலர்கள்` retain the repeated **flowers of his eyes / feet / hands** imagery;
- `தமிழ்த் தாய்` → **Mother Tamil**;
- `கழகம்` → **Kazhagam**, avoiding an over-specific modern party substitution;
- `சமதர்மப் பூ` → **the flower of social equality**;
- `நெற்கதிர் போல் தலைநாணச்` → **make their heads bow like ripened ears of paddy**;
- `பூ முடியே ! புகழ்முடியே !` → **O flower crown ... O crown of fame!**;
- `பாடிவரும் வண்டாக` → **a bee that comes singing**;
