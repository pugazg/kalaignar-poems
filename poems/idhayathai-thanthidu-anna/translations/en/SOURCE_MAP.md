# English Translation Source Map — இதயத்தைத் தந்திடு அண்ணா

## Purpose

This map ties every planned English translation batch back to the verified Tamil source records and reviewed Tamil assembly.

The map is navigational. It does **not** replace the Tamil page files or the controlling scan.

## Source hierarchy

For translation decisions, use this order:

1. supplied source scan — ultimate authority if a Tamil reading is reopened;
2. verified page records `../../pages/0013.md`–`../../pages/0026.md`;
3. reviewed Tamil assembly `../../sections/idhayathai-thanthidu-anna.md`;
4. `../../ASSEMBLY_REVIEW.md` and `../../audit.md` for recorded fidelity decisions.

No secondary English/Tamil text may silently override this hierarchy.

## Cross-page continuity points

These continuations must be checked during translation so that page boundaries do not cause omissions, duplication or false punctuation.

### Scan 13 → 14

Scan 13 ends:

`நாடிவரும் பூமுடியே! புகழ் முடியே! உமைத்`

Scan 14 begins:

`தேடிவரும் வாழ்த்துக் குவியலிலே - தினம்`

The English should preserve the grammatical/rhetorical continuation while keeping the source map traceable.

### Scan 14 → 15

Scan 14 ends:

`கவிப்பரணியேற்றி, காஞ்சிபுரப் பரணி`

Scan 15 begins:

`பாடி நின்றேன் இன்று`

Treat the continuation as one rhetorical movement across the physical boundary.

### Scan 17 → 18

Scan 17 ends:

`வயிறு தாங்காக் காரணத்தால்`

Scan 18 begins:

`தனித்தனித் தாய் ஈன்றெடுத்த தம்பிகளே! என அழைத்துக்`

Do not translate either fragment as an independent completed thought.

### Scan 18 → 19

Scan 18 ends:

`அன்புறு காந்தியின் அருளால் இன்று`

Scan 19 begins:

`எழுந்தனர் அடிமைத் தளையினை அறுத்து!`

This continuation crosses the Batch 02 internal page boundary and must remain semantically continuous.

### Scan 22 → 23

Scan 22 ends:

`‘தமிழர் மனம் வாழ்வெல்லாம்`

Scan 23 begins:

`தங்கமாக ஆக்க’ என்றாள்.`

This is a quotation continuation. Preserve that quotation logic in English.

### Scan 23 → 24

Scan 23 ends:

`பதிகத்துப் பொருளாய்ப்`

Scan 24 begins:

`பழந்தமிழர் புறப்பாட்டாய்`

This continuation crosses Batch 04 → Batch 05. Batch 05 drafting must reread the final rhetorical unit of Batch 04 before translating its opening.

### Scan 24 → 25

Scan 24 ends the statue sequence with:

`கம்பர்க்கும் சிலை`

Scan 25 continues:

`தீரமாய்க் கப்பலோட்டிய தமிழர்க்கும் சிலை`

Preserve the catalogue/crescendo rather than treating scan 25 as a fresh topic.

## Batch 01 source notes — scans 13–15

Key fidelity features to watch:

- direct praise and apostrophe to Anna;
- botanical/body imagery: petals, flowers, fruit, eyes, hands, feet;
- martial and literary imagery;
- `கேண்மினோ!` / `கேண்மின்!` as public-oratorical calls;
- transition from praise to `அவலப் பரணி`;
- `வெள்ளம்! / வெள்ளம்! / மாபெரும் வெள்ளம்!` must retain its repetition and escalation;
- unusual verified source forms must not be “corrected” before translation.

## Batch 02 source notes — scans 16–19

Key fidelity features:

- repeated `முன்றெழுத்து` structure is a central rhetorical device;
- quoted Anna sayings must remain compact and quotational;
- political-history narration accelerates into the Periyar/Anna split and movement formation;
- scan 19 contains stepped indentation and repeated emotional address to the gathered public;
- preserve rhetorical repetition rather than summarize historical events.

## Batch 03 source notes — scans 20–21

Key fidelity features:

- praise by public figures and the poem's movement through named historical persons;
- public/governance claims are part of Kalaignar's rhetoric and must not be neutralized;
- scan 21 turns from praise into Tamil literary memory and social critique;
- `மலர் / மன்னன் / நூல்` sequence should retain its compact parallelism.

## Batch 04 source notes — scans 22–23

Key fidelity features:

- catalogue of social decline and moral vocabulary;
- personification of Tamil mother;
- dialogue with Valluvar depends on quotation, question/answer rhythm and literary allusion;
- unusual quotation marks in Tamil are a source feature; English quotation style may be regularized only at the typographic level, not by changing who speaks or what is said;
- final lines continue directly into Batch 05.

## Batch 05 source notes — scans 24–26

Key fidelity features:

- accumulative list of Anna's roles and descriptions;
- statue/cultural-memory catalogue;
- sharp turn into grief at the one-finger image;
- repeated direct questions must stay direct;
- `எம் அண்ணா.. இதயமன்னா...` and the closing pleas require emotional and rhythmic restraint — do not over-embellish;
- final heart request is the poem's title-bearing emotional resolution and must retain its directness.

## Material excluded from poem batches

### Scans 27–28

Back matter is excluded from this poem translation. Scan 27 contains a separate Kalaignar composition associated with the World Classical Tamil Conference and must be treated as a separate composition if translated later.

## Source-map review rule

Before a batch is marked reviewed, confirm:

- every source scan in the batch is represented;
- no adjacent continuation point is lost;
- no line from a neighbouring batch is duplicated;
- notes identify any intentionally non-literal English solution;
- Kalaignar's repetition, direct address and rhetorical movement remain visible.
