## Full-poem consistency decisions

- `Anna` remains the governing relationship/title word.
- `Ayya`, `Kazhagam`, `Dravidar Kazhagam`, `Munnetra Kazhagam`, `Muttamil`, `Parani`, `Tirukkural/Kural`, `Navalar`, `pathigam` and `purappāṭṭu` remain visible where culturally or rhetorically active.
- Transliteration is functional rather than mechanically academic: difficult/source-sensitive forms remain visible; familiar names stay readable.
- Repetition remains intact, including `A flood! / A flood! / A mighty flood!`, repeated `three letters`, repeated `They call him...`, paired public questions, Mother Tamil's repeated actions and `You will not come; you will not come;`.
- The opening flower/body frame returns at the end in `foot-flowers`.
- The title-bearing closing request keeps the temporary borrowing/return logic of Anna's heart.
