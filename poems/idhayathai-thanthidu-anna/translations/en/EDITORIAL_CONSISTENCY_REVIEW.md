### Transliteration policy

The translation does **not** impose one academic transliteration system across every Tamil word. Transliteration is functional:

- diacritics are used where preserving the Tamil lexical form is part of wordplay or a difficult literary term (`vāzhvu`, `kādhal`, `veṟṟi`, `purappāṭṭu`);
- familiar public names/titles remain in readable conventional Roman forms (`Anna`, `Kazhagam`, `Tirukkural`);
- unresolved/difficult verified forms remain visibly transliterated where guessing would violate source authority (`tangu sani vēl`, `kazhar-chchenni`, `sazhakkar`).

This mixed policy is deliberate and documented, not accidental inconsistency.

Result: **PASS**.

### Agricultural imagery

### Tongue / finger / writing imagery
