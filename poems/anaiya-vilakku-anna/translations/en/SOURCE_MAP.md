# English Translation Source Map — அணையா விளக்கு அண்ணா

## Purpose

This map ties every planned English translation batch back to the verified Tamil source records and reviewed Tamil assembly.

The map is navigational. It does **not** replace the Tamil page files or the controlling scan.

## Source hierarchy

For translation decisions, use this order:

1. supplied source scan — ultimate authority if a Tamil reading is reopened;
2. verified page records `../../pages/0007.md`–`../../pages/0017.md`;
3. reviewed Tamil assembly `../../sections/anaiya-vilakku-anna.md`;
4. `../../ASSEMBLY_REVIEW.md` and `../../audit.md` for recorded fidelity decisions.

No secondary English/Tamil text may silently override this hierarchy.

## Cross-page continuity points

### Scan 8 → 9

Scan 8 closes the electricity/political-satire movement by turning explicitly toward Anna and ends:

`அண்ணனே! / எங்கள் உயிரே! / உணர்வே!`

Scan 9 immediately continues the apostrophic praise:

`இனமானக் கோவே! / எழுச்சியின் வடிவே!`

Do not treat scan 9 as an unrelated fresh poem.

### Scan 9 → 10

Scan 9 ends in the historical claim that Tamil people came to understand their full history after Anna's arrival. Scan 10 continues the cultural/political-historical movement beginning with the transformation of `வருஷங்கள்` into `ஆண்டுகள்` and Tamil New Year rhetoric.

The English should preserve the argumentative continuation without inventing bridging prose.

### Scan 10 → 11

Scan 10 ends with the claim that Anna gave Kalaignar the strength to condemn the removal of Kannagi's statue. Scan 11 turns directly to Tolkappiyam's classification of forms of knowledge and Kalaignar's rationalist exposition. Preserve the continuity of Anna as the enabling intellectual/political presence.

### Scan 11 → 12

Scan 11 ends mid-argument:

`தாருகா வனத்து ரிஷி பத்தினிகளிடம் தவ முனிவர்கள்`

Scan 12 begins:

`கூட்டணி வைத்து, கொட்டம் அடிப்பது தான் இரக்கம் என்று / பொருள் கூறுவீரோ?`

This is one continuing rhetorical question. Do not close the thought at the batch/page boundary.

### Scan 12 → 13

Scan 12 ends:

`வறிஞன் செல்வன் என்று`

Scan 13 begins:

`வரையறுக்கும் வாக்கு பேதம் / வளர்த்தல் தீதென்று சமதர்ம / வழி நின்று உரைத்தவன் நீ!`

This is a direct continuation and crosses Batch 02 → Batch 03. Batch 03 must reread scan 12's final unit before drafting.

### Scan 13 → 14

Scan 13 ends with Anna's remembered statement about Kalaignar:

`கருணாநிதி என்று சொன்னாய்-`

Scan 14 continues the personal/movement memory with Kalaignar's Palayamkottai imprisonment and Anna's response. Preserve the remembered-speech/memory sequence.

### Scan 14 → 15

Scan 14 ends with questions about Tamil courage:

`எங்குற்றான் என் தமிழன்? / செங்குத்துத் தூணைப் போல் நிற்கின்றானா? / அங்கத்தில் துடிப்பின்றிக் கிடக்கின்றானா?`

Scan 15 answers/extends this martial-cultural rhetoric through the Chera/Chola/Pandya moustache imagery and the demand for renewed valour before moving to the rice-policy passage.

### Scan 15 → 16

Scan 15 ends inside a long quoted imagined utterance from Anna:

`அவனை மகிழ்ச்சியாக வாரி அணைத்து வாழ்த்துகின்றேன்” -`

Scan 16 begins:

`இது கண்டு - / வாயிலும் வயிற்றிலும் அடித்துக் கொண்டு.....`

The quoted passage has closed, but the satirical political reaction continues. This boundary also crosses Batch 03 → Batch 04.

### Scan 16 → 17

Scan 16's `முடிவுரை` ends its page with:

`மத நல்லிணக்கம் விரும்பிப் போற்றி`

Scan 17 continues:

`மனித நேயம் மதிக்கின்ற இடத்திலேயே இருந்து விட்டேன் -`

This is one grammatical/rhetorical continuation and must not be translated as two independent statements.

## Batch 01 source notes — scans 7–9

Key fidelity features:

- ceremonial greeting/address to assembled groups and dignitaries;
- wordplay around electricity, household life and candles;
- contemporary political satire and explicit references to Kudankulam, the Prime Minister and Sonia;
- `(பலத்த கைதட்டல்)` is a source-visible performance cue;
- sharp transition from satire to direct invocation of Anna;
- repeated centenary/lifespan refrain on scan 9 must remain repeated;
- short praise lines rely on accumulation and cadence rather than explanatory syntax.

## Batch 02 source notes — scans 10–12

Key fidelity features:

- Tamil linguistic/cultural and Dravidian historical rhetoric;
- named references including Sundaranar, Nehru, Periyar, Tolkappiyam, Sangam Tamil, Kural, *Poompuhar*, *Kamba Rasam* and *Romapuri Rani*;
- six-level knowledge classification must remain intelligible without turning verse into textbook prose;
- sustained rationalist interrogation of Ramayana/religious narratives;
- rhetorical questions must remain questions;
- repeated centenary refrain returns on scan 12;
- final line crosses into Batch 03.

## Batch 03 source notes — scans 13–15

Key fidelity features:

- Anna Renaissance Scheme/development rhetoric and public-policy references;
- Salem steel, Sethusamudram and classical-language status for Tamil;
- personal/movement loyalty and remembered statements by Anna;
- emotionally charged betrayal/forgiveness passage, including `மறப்போம்; மன்னிப்போம்`;
- Tamil martial imagery through Chera/Chola/Pandya moustaches must not be rationalized away;
- transition from valour to welfare/policy rhetoric;
- long quoted rice-policy passage must preserve speaker distinction and source cadence.

## Batch 04 source notes — scans 16–17

Key fidelity features:

- satirical reaction at the start of scan 16;
- `கழகம் சரணம் கச்சாமி!` is deliberate rhetorical wordplay and should not be flattened;
- internal `★ ★ ★` separator and `முடிவுரை` heading must remain structurally visible;
- political discussion of left parties, central government, Sonia, secularism, humanism and religious communalism must remain specific;
- final Tamil-language demand and public call must retain speech cadence;
- concluding `மாநில சுயாட்சி; / மத்தியிலே கூட்டாட்சி!` is a paired slogan;
- event caption/date after the second `★ ★ ★` is contextual source matter, not poem verse.

## Source-map review rule

Before a batch is marked reviewed, confirm:

- every source scan in the batch is represented;
- no adjacent continuation point is lost;
- no line from a neighbouring batch is duplicated;
- notes identify any intentionally non-literal English solution;
- Kalaignar's repetition, direct address, satire, political specificity and rhetorical movement remain visible;
- no translation choice silently modifies the locked Tamil source.
